# Domain glossary: diffusion language models

The vocabulary of reports on language models that generate text by masked diffusion instead of left-to-right decoding: the diffusion process, its masking and denoising steps and its decoding schedules. General model and training terms (mô hình sinh, mô hình khuếch tán, huấn luyện, tinh chỉnh, giải mã) come from the common layer; model names (LLaDA-8B-Base, LLaDA-8B-Instruct) stay as written.
