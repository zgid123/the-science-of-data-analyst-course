# Topic glossary: course completion and early contact in online and blended courses

The vocabulary of a Milestone 1 analytics proposal (MTH045) on low course completion and mid-semester disengagement in
a university's online and blended courses, with AI-simulated stakeholder interviews. Terms that the course brief writes
in English stay in English. Vietnamese forms follow what a Vietnamese lecturer or academic-affairs officer would say
(guide section 4, rule 8): plain, everyday words of the field, not mirrors of the English.
