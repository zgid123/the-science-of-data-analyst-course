# Topic glossary: course completion and early contact in online and blended courses

The proposal vocabulary of team N04's MTH045 project on low course completion and mid-semester disengagement in a university's online and blended courses: the courses and their outcomes, the university's systems and staff, the early signs and contact process, the goals, KPIs and analytical questions, and the stakeholder interviews behind them. Terms that the course brief writes in English stay in English. Vietnamese forms follow what a Vietnamese lecturer or academic-affairs officer would say (guide section 4, rule 8): plain, everyday words of the field, not mirrors of the English. The project's other topic files hold the OULAD data vocabulary (oulad-data-preparation), the prediction and evaluation vocabulary (early-warning-evaluation) and the course brief's labels, AI roles and running-text wording (course-completion-report-conventions); a term is defined in one of these files only.
