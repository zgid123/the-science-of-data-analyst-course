### 3.4.2. Adoption and Enforcement

The mandate reached the population quickly. According to SBV statistics reported on 3 July 2024, "more than 13 million customers have completed facial biometric verification in online transactions over the first two days following the implementation of Decision No.2345/2023" [99]. Transactions above VND 10 million made up 8.24% of transactions in that period [99]. The first days also exposed practical obstacles, including customers without chip-based citizen identity cards, phones that could not read the chip, network congestion and failed recognition [99].

Enforcement followed the dates set in Section 3.4.1. By the end of October 2024, the Deputy Governor reported about 37 million successful biometric registrations [94]. From 1 January 2025, holders of accounts without biometric verification could no longer withdraw, transfer or pay by card online, while in-person card payments at point-of-sale terminals continued [94]. The SBV then directed the sector to match the biometrics of every payment account used on digital channels [100]. As of 15 August 2025, more than 123.9 million individual customer records had been matched, covering all individual payment accounts with digital-channel transactions, together with more than 1.3 million organisational records [100]. By August 2025, 57 credit institutions and 39 payment intermediaries read the chip-based identity card in their mobile apps [100].

The SBV links the programme to a fall in fraud: "số vụ lừa đảo giảm hơn 59%, các tài khoản liên quan đến gian lận giảm 52% so với trước thời điểm thực hiện đối chiếu thông tin sinh trắc học", that is, fraud cases fell by more than 59% and fraud-related accounts by 52% compared with the period before biometric matching [100]. The most recent figure comes from the Governor's report to the National Assembly: as of 11 September 2026, more than 173 million individual and more than 2.89 million organisational customer records had been matched through the chip-based identity card or the VNeID application [101].
