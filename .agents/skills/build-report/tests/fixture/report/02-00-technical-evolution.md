# 2. Evolution of the Field in Technical Systems

Part 1 classified PAD by evidence source, user role and exploited cue. This part orders the same methods in time and shows how each generation of detectors answered the weakness of the one before. The history falls into three eras, each dated by the paradigm that dominated it rather than by hard boundaries, since new approaches appeared while older ones were still being refined.

Section 2.1 covers Era 1, in which hand-crafted texture, quality, motion and blink cues were tuned to small datasets and failed across them. Section 2.2 covers Era 2, in which deep classifiers with auxiliary depth and rPPG supervision, and later central difference convolution, reached low errors within datasets on larger benchmarks. Section 2.3 covers Era 3, in which domain generalisation and pretrained vision-language models reduced the error on unseen datasets while the threat itself moved from the sensor to digital injection and deepfakes. Section 2.4 summarises the three eras in one table.
