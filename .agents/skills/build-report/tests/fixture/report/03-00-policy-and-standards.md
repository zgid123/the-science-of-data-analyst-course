# 3. Evolution in Policy, Standards and Government Mandates

Part 2 traced what researchers built; this part traces what institutions required. The same concepts that Part 1 defined, presentation attacks, PAI species and the APCER and BPCER error rates, moved from research papers into standards, laboratory tests, government benchmarks and national law, each on its own institutional timeline.

Section 3.1 follows the international standard ISO/IEC 30107 and the schemes built on it. Section 3.2 follows the commercial test laboratory iBeta, whose confirmation letters turned the standard into a market document. Section 3.3 follows the NIST benchmark that compared 82 algorithms on sequestered data. Section 3.4 follows Vietnam's biometric banking mandate, in which a PAD requirement became binding law for a national population. Section 3.5 then compares the four kinds of institution.
