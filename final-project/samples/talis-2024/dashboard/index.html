TALIS 2024: assessment practices, training needs and survey design

Assessment-practice context only: cross-sectional teachers/principals, without student assessment-event or future-outcome linkage. Country/system and ISCED estimates remain separate. Rating 0 adjudication is insufficient for national inference.

Read the reportReproduce and openVerification and review

All country/level estimates use valid responses and positive teacher weights; no global pooled percentage. An unavailable combination is missing, not zero. Public outputs contain no teacher identifiers.

Which assessment methods and feedback are reported?

Practice success means frequently/always (codes 3/4); need success means moderate/high need. Intervals use 100 Fay-BRR replicates, factor 0.5, and are exploratory normal 95% intervals. Adjudication: 0 insufficient, 1 poor, 2 fair, 3 good.

Compare feedback methods on the same respondents

Paired differences retain covariance and common-response denominators. An interval containing zero does not establish a difference or prove equivalence.

Does frequent feedback remove training needs?

This is an exploratory, cross-sectional association on common valid item responses. Small less-frequent-feedback groups have wider uncertainty; no causal effect or student-risk outcome is measured.

Missing by design is not absence of practice

Form coverage below is a fixed full-level reference and does not follow the country filter. Zero valid responses on form 1 are not teachers reporting zero feedback. Naively coding all absent responses as zero is shown only as an invalid sensitivity contrast.

Population coverage and principal-file scope

Teacher level extracts equal the combined export on all selected items and respondent keys. Use either representation once. Principal files contain school-level context; they cannot create the absent student assessment/outcome linkage.
