PISA 2022: feedback and assessment records before warning analytics

Local school/teacher questionnaires provide assessment-practice context, without student assessment-event or later-outcome linkage. Mathematics/general-teacher domains stay separate. Historical ESCS export covers 2012/2015/2018, not 2022 student outcomes.

Country controls teacher and school views. Type controls only teacher views. Optional teacher participation means some countries have school data but no teacher-domain observations. Empty rates stay undefined.

Which feedback leaves a potential assessment record?

Teacher practices: most/every lesson, codes 3/4. Need: moderate/high. Final teacher weights and 80 Fay-BRR replicates, factor 0.5; exploratory normal intervals. Reported feedback does not establish student-event availability or a causal effect.

Compare feedback on the same teachers

Common-response difference avoids attributing item-sample differences to practice. General and mathematics-teacher domains are not pooled.

Does a reported recording policy supply student histories?

School points use grade-nonresponse-adjusted school base weights and are descriptive. School replicate weights were not supplied, so no design interval is claimed. Reports of recording or adapting instruction cannot replace actual student-level logs.

Exploratory common-response association. Feedback frequency, training need and student academic outcomes are different concepts; there is no causal or early-warning target here.

Who and what years are actually observed?

Historical trend coverage is a fixed full-export reference, independent of country/type filters. ESCS rows have no achievement outcome or survey weights and cannot be relabeled 2022 student records.
