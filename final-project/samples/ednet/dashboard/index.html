Who has enough future practice to be evaluated?

50,000 users sampled uniformly from 784,309 local user CSVs; six million sampled response rows. Users with insufficient follow-up are unlabeled, not adverse outcomes. Overlap exclusions require future questions after estimated prefix completion.

Does behavior improve on prior accuracy?

Each interval compares combined minus score AP using a paired held-out-user bootstrap. All three intervals include zero: added behavior value is not demonstrated. All models share early part-7 exposure context; adaptive question difficulty remains incompletely controlled.

Does the warning leave time to act?

First-future-question intervals are often seconds. Time to the end of the entire outcome window is a different quantity and does not establish time to prevent its earliest difficulty. Fixed 20% reference; durations approximate completion from presentation timestamp plus elapsed_time.

How many flags are true later difficulties?

False positives are users above the proposed future-accuracy threshold; they are not confirmed academically safe. Test outcomes do not choose the alert threshold. Most reference flags are false positives against the <40% proxy.

Challenge the target, repetition and sampling stability

Novel-future-item labels omit questions seen in the prefix and require ten novel responses. The 50% threshold is a different proposed proxy. Sample-half variability limits confidence in small improvements; no model is selected on these challenges.

Content-related errors and probability reliability

Future dominant question part is an audit label, never a predictor. Fixed 20% reference for errors; calibration is independent of threshold. No demographic attributes or official academic outcomes were supplied.
