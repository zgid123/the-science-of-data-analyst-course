# List of Terms

| Term | Meaning in this report |
| :---- | :---- |
| Add-drop period | The first weeks of a semester in which students may still register for or drop a course; the population of the project counts enrolments after it (Section 1.2) |
| Blended course | A course that combines online lessons on the LMS with in-class sessions (Section 1.1) |
| Contact record | A shared log of who contacted which student, when, how and with what result; a new data requirement (Section 6.1) |
| Course completion | Passing the course, also called successful course completion; a fail or a course withdrawal counts as non-completion (Section 3.3) |
| Data cut-off | The last day whose data may be used for a list: the end of week 2 for the week-3 list and the end of week 4 for the week-5 list (Section 4.2) |
| Early signs | Signals of disengagement observable in weeks 1–4, such as missed or late tasks, that AQ2 compares (Section 4.1) |
| Early-sign rule | Two or more assessed tasks missed or late by the week-4 data cut-off; the basis of the first secondary KPI (Section 3.3) |
| Lecturer-advisor | A lecturer who also acts as academic advisor (cố vấn học tập) for one or two classes of students (Section 2.3) |
| Persona | The role description, grounded in published accounts, that an AI followed when answering as a stakeholder (Section 2.2) |
| Pilot courses | The online and blended courses chosen for the first semester of the process, compared with similar non-pilot courses (Section 3.2) |
| Precision | The share of students on a list who later fail or withdraw (Section 4.1) |
| Recall | The share of students who later fail or withdraw who appear on a list (Section 4.1) |
| Review point | A moment in the semester when a contact list reaches staff: week 3 and week 5 (Section 4.1) |
| Validation round | The return to each stakeholder with the team's understanding, to confirm, correct and add (Section 2.4) |
