## 6.2. Feasibility, Quality Risks and Privacy

The data needs of Table 6.1 are feasible for a small pilot: the data office can support a few courses with a
consistent setup once legal approval is given, while routine linking and any automated step must wait (VDO4, VDO5).
Table 6.2 lists the main data risks and how the project handles them.

**Table 6.2. Data quality and feasibility risks**

| Risk | Source | Handling |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Status mixes leave and transfers with withdrawal | DO3, VPM2; [4] | Recount baseline years under Circular 83/2026 (VPM8) |
| One LMS measure means different things across courses | DO2 | Compare within course; prefer assessment records (VDO1) |
| Activity is not learning; logins hide disengagement | CL3, VST1; [22] | Missed and late tasks as main signs; staff check each name |
| Manual matching, one-week delay | DO1, DO4 | Weeks 2 and 4 data feed the week-3 and week-5 lists (VDO3) |
| Small groups give unstable rates and can identify students | Group comparisons in AQ2 and AQ3 | Group results only above an agreed minimum size |

Privacy and ethics set firm limits. Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Law, in force since 1 January 2026, ties
consent to a stated purpose and does not treat silence as consent [18], and the Ministry of Education requires
universities to secure education databases and report breaches within 72 hours [23]. Following codes of practice,
the project applies five rules: (1) the purpose, data, access and use are explained to staff and students [11]; (2) students are told at the start of the course and consulted on how their data are used [22]; (3) consent is sought for personal
interventions [11]; (4) access is limited to staff with a legitimate need [11]; and (5) the data have an agreed
lifespan [22]. Results are never used for grading, profiling or judging lecturers [11]. Once the university knows that
a student is struggling, it has an obligation to act [22], so a list never exceeds the capacity to follow it up (DO10).
No real student data are entered into public AI tools [1].
