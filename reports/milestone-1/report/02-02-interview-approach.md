## 2.2. Interview Approach

Generative AI took both sides of each interview, following the procedure of the brief [1]. An isolated AI persona, a
conversation that knew only its role and the scenario and whose traits come from published accounts of students,
tutors and advisors and from the Vietnamese rules on withdrawal and personal data [3], [12–18], answered as the
stakeholder; an AI interviewer acting for us asked seven or eight prepared open questions one at a time, with
follow-ups, in a separate conversation for each stakeholder, and we labelled 51 key statements and verified every
transcript. Appendix VI describes the method step by step, and Appendices I and II give the questions and the
statements.
