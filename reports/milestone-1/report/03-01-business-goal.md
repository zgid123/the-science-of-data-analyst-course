## 3.1. Business Goal

The project's Business Goal is to **improve successful course completion in the program's online and blended courses by identifying students who stop taking part early enough to support them within the semester**. It names an
outcome for the organisation, more students passing their courses, not an activity such as analysing LMS data.

It comes from the program manager, who wants to know early how many students really complete rather than see a
better-looking rate (PM1, PM2), the support office, which wants to reach students before the warning list (AD1), and the lecturer (CL2). It
matters to students, for whom a failed course costs time and money, and to the university, whose withdrawal rate is
tied to enrolment quotas [3]. It supports two decisions in each semester: whom staff contact first, and where the
program manager places advisors' time (Section 7).
