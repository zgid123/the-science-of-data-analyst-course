# Team interview question lists (Task 2)

Written by the team before any interview (T08, 2026-11-05). Each list has 5–8 open questions covering the five
information groups of the brief (Task 2): Business Goals (BG), Success Vision (SV), Challenges (CH), Decision Making (DM),
Risk Anticipation (RA). Follow-up questions are added during each interview and recorded in its transcript.

Codes: PM Program Manager, CL Course Lecturer, AD Academic Advisor / Student Support Officer, ST Student,
DO Learning Technology and Data Officer.

## Leading-question self-check

Every question was read against the brief's warning ("Anh/chị có nghĩ thời gian xem video thấp là nguyên nhân sinh viên
bỏ học không?" imposes an assumption). Rules applied: no cause named in the question; no data source, metric or tool
suggested; no yes/no form; "at risk", "dashboard", "prediction" and "AI" do not appear until the stakeholder brings
them up. Two drafts were rewritten:

| Draft | Problem | Final |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| CL: "Would an early-warning dashboard of LMS activity help you spot students who are falling behind?" | Proposes a solution and a data source; yes/no | CL-Q5: "If you had better information about how your students are doing during the semester, what would you do differently, and when?" |
| ST: "Did low online activity cause you to fall behind?" | Names a cause; yes/no | ST-Q2: "Can you tell me about a time you, or a classmate, started to fall behind or stopped attending a course? What was going on?" |
