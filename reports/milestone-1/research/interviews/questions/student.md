# ST – Student

| ID | Group | Question |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| ST-Q1 | BG | What matters most to you when you take an online or blended course? |
| ST-Q2 | CH | Can you tell me about a time you, or a classmate, started to fall behind or stopped attending a course? What was going on? |
| ST-Q3 | CH | Looking back, when did it start, and who noticed – you, a friend, a lecturer, anyone at the university? |
| ST-Q4 | SV | What would have helped you, or your classmate, finish that course? |
| ST-Q5 | DM | If the university noticed you were falling behind, how would you want them to contact you, and who should it be? |
| ST-Q6 | RA | What would you think if you learned the university used your study activity in the online system to decide whom to contact? |
| ST-Q7 | RA | What would make such contact feel helpful, and what would make it feel unwelcome? |
