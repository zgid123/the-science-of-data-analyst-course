# Stakeholder map – initial hypotheses (Task 1, before the interviews)

Written by the team on 2026-11-05 (T08). Goals and decisions are hypotheses that the interviews test (brief Task 1.3).

| Code | Stakeholder | Role in the problem | Initial assumed goal | Decision possibly supported |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| PM | Academic Program Manager / Program Coordinator | Owns program quality and completion figures; allocates teaching and support resources | Raise the share of students who complete their courses in the program's online and blended courses | Where to put support resources each semester; which courses to review or redesign |
| CL | Course Lecturer / Course Coordinator | Runs the course; first to see who stops attending or submitting | Keep students taking part until the end of the course and passing it | Whom to contact or remind, and when; which activities or deadlines to adjust |
| AD | Academic Advisor / Student Support Officer | Contacts and supports students; limited time per student | Reach students who need help early enough for help to work | Which students to contact first and with what kind of support |
| ST | Student (affected party) | Takes the course; is the subject of any data use and contact | Finish the course while balancing work, family and other courses | Whether to accept help, change study load, or withdraw in time |
| DO | Learning Technology and Data Officer (LMS administration, academic affairs data) | Holds and joins LMS and academic-record data; applies privacy rules | Make data usable for staff without breaching privacy or overloading systems | Which data may be shared with whom, how often, and under which safeguards |

Why these five: the brief requires PM, CL and AD (section 4). The student is the person every decision acts on and the
source of the privacy and trust concerns (brief Task 9 note). The data officer decides whether the data needs of Task 9
can be met at all, so the proposal's feasibility rests on that view.
