**Interviewer (validation):** [validation-brief.md, points 1–5] Which of these points can your office support next semester, and which would have to wait?

**DO:** You have a lot right. Missed quizzes and assignments are a better early sign than clicks, and the LMS does record them. I also agree with point 4, that results are for support only and a person makes the final judgement. A few corrections. On point 4, telling students isn't enough. As I understand the law, consent is given for each purpose and silence isn't consent, and legal has to approve linking LMS data to status records first. Keeping status records correct also needs a shared process between academic affairs, student affairs and my unit, and from 15 November that's a compliance matter. On point 5, I can't confirm the 70% baseline, and a completion rate only means something once leave and transfers are separated from real dropout. As for what's missing: today the matching is manual, each course is set up differently, I have no dedicated budget, and I'm not sure our systems can handle students who opt out. With exports done by hand, a week-5 list could already be a week old. As for next semester: assuming legal signs off in time, I think my office could support a small pilot. That would mean exporting missed quizzes and assignments for a few courses whose lecturers agree to a consistent setup, and checking status records with academic affairs. Routine linking across the whole programme, and anything automated, would have to wait.
