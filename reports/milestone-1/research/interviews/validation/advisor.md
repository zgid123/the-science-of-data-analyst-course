# Validation round: advisor

Same conversation as transcripts/advisor.md, continued after the interview (T11, 2026-11-05). The team presents its understanding (validation-brief.md) and asks the persona to confirm, correct and add; answers are written here verbatim by the persona agent.

**Interviewer (validation):** [validation-brief.md, points 1–5] Given your weekly capacity, how would you want the list split between your office and the lecturer-advisors?

**AD:** You've got most of it right: the short list with a reason for each name, contact from someone the student knows, a supportive tone, and the judgement staying with a person. One correction to point 1: we don't notice nothing until the end. Lecturers do email me sometimes during the semester; it's just scattered, late and unrecorded. On timing, please remember week 5 can collide with registration or exams; a list that lands then gets ignored. What's missing: closing the loop with the lecturer who raised the concern, otherwise they stop telling us; a simple shared record of who was contacted and what happened, because without it I can't tell you anything at the end; and the fact that a student drifting in several courses at once is a different case from one course. On point 5, I'm wary of a completion target. The students who answer us may have finished anyway, so I'd be careful claiming the five points as our doing. As for the split, this is my own view: the lecturer-advisors should take students from their own classes, because students answer people they know, but only a handful each, three to five a week. My office should take the harder cases: students missing across several courses, repeat warnings, and anyone whose advisor couldn't reach them after two tries. Three of us can manage maybe thirty to forty a week between us, at a guess.
