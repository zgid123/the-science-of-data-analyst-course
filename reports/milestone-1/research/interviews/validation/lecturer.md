# Validation round: lecturer

Same conversation as transcripts/lecturer.md, continued after the interview (T11, 2026-11-05). The team presents its understanding (validation-brief.md) and asks the persona to confirm, correct and add; answers are written here verbatim by the persona agent.

**Interviewer (validation):** [validation-brief.md, points 1–5] Would a list in week 5 come early enough for your course, and what reason would you need to see next to each name?

**CL:** Most of this rings true. Points 2, 3 and 4 are right, especially keeping it supportive and never using it to judge lecturers. One correction on point 1: I don't only notice after the semester. I usually notice during it, by hand, but around week five or six, and with 250 students I miss some. The problem is that it's late and patchy, not that nobody sees it. Also, in my view, a silent student isn't always a disengaged one; some work offline or have poor internet, so please keep that in the wording. On the goal: completion is fine, but I'd want it to mean finishing having done the work, not just a pass. And the 70% baseline, you've said it's assumed; I can't confirm it for my course. What's missing for me: a simple record of who was contacted, by whom, and what happened, including whether student support followed up a referral. Otherwise I'm back to my spreadsheet. And the TAs' time, since they'd do much of the contacting. Now your question. Week 5 is borderline for my course. In my experience we start losing them between the second and third assignment, so I'd rather have a first look around week 2 or 3, and then a refresh a few days before each assignment deadline, not one list once. Next to each name I'd need the concrete reason: which quizzes or assignments are missing or late, and since when. Not a score, the actual facts, so I know what to say when I write.
