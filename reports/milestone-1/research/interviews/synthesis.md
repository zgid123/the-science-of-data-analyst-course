## 4. Refine

Problem statement: in the program's online and blended courses, students who stop taking part are identified only
after the semester ends, although their disengagement usually begins within the first five weeks; advisors and
lecturers can follow up only a few dozen students a week and have no shared, timely and trusted information on whom
to contact first and why.

Out of scope (refined away): changing assessment or course design during the semester (CL7: fixed); admissions and
quota policy; financial aid; evaluating lecturers (CL9); students' circumstances outside the course (PM6: cannot fix);
automated decisions about students (AD9).

## 5. Team proposal taken to the validation round (draft)

- Business Goal: improve successful course completion in the program's online and blended courses by identifying
  students who stop taking part early enough to support them within the semester.
- SMART Goal (assumed figures): raise the course completion rate of students enrolled after the add-drop period in the
  selected online and blended courses from an assumed 70% to 75% by the end of Semester 2, 2026–2027.
- Primary KPI: course completion rate. Secondary KPIs: early-contact coverage (share of students who later fail or
  withdraw who were contacted by week 6); follow-up rate (share of listed students contacted by a named staff member
  within 7 days of listing).
- Analytic pathway: describe completion and the timing of disengagement → compare early indicators of completers and
  non-completers → estimate non-completion at week 5 → decide whom to contact first within weekly capacity.
