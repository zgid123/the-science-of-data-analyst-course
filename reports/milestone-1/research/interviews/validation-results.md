## Validation table (brief Task 4)

| Validation content | Result |
| :---- | :---- |
| Points the stakeholders confirmed | A short, ordered list with a factual reason per name (all five); contact by someone who knows the student, supportive wording, never "at risk" (CL, AD, ST); support-only use with the final judgement kept by a person (PM, CL, DO); missed quizzes and assignments as a better early sign than LMS activity (DO); the revised SMART Goal (VPM7) |
| Points the stakeholders asked to adjust | Detection is late and patchy, not absent (VCL1, VAD1); review points at week 3 and week 5, outside registration and exam weeks (VCL2, VAD2); timing of disengagement stated as a stakeholder view (VPM1); completion means passing (VPM6); target over the 2027–2028 academic year, baseline recounted under Circular 83 (VPM5, VPM8); consent per purpose and legal approval before linking (VDO2); family and employers never informed (VST2) |
| Points added | Program-level view of courses and groups (VPM3); first-year students as a segment (VPM4); contact record and closing the loop with the lecturer (VCL4, VAD1); multi-course disengagement and routing between lecturer-advisors and the support office (VAD3, VAD5); a simple "I'm fine" reply (VST3); pilot scope and comparison with non-pilot courses (VDO4, VPM9) |
| Points the team did not adopt, and why | (1) AD's caution against a completion target (VAD4): the brief requires an organisational outcome as the Business Goal, so completion stays the primary KPI, reported as progress and not as the effect of contact. (2) CL's wish that completion mean "having done the work" (CL2): passing is the outcome the program reports; the trail of submitted work is kept as a descriptive measure in AQ1. (3) Some students prefer a general email to everyone (ST, persona's classmates): personal contact stays the default because AD and ST report that generic emails are ignored (AD8); a general notice of available support goes to every student at course start |
