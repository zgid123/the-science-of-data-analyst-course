# Shared scenario given to every simulated stakeholder

Source: brief section 3 [S001], translated by the team; the location is a scenario assumption.

"A university in Vietnam (scenario assumption) is expanding online and blended learning. In several courses, the
university has noticed signs that students take part less, or do not complete the course as expected. The university
may hold data from its learning management system (LMS), its academic management system, assignments, quizzes, grades
and surveys; however, it has not yet decided which questions should be analysed, or which decisions analytics should
support. The Data Analytics Team is gathering requirements before any analysis starts."

## Interviewer opening prompt (brief section 4.2, English rendering, used verbatim for every persona)

"You play the role of [ROLE] at a university. Context: [SCENARIO ABOVE]. I am a Data Analyst carrying out requirement
gathering before a data analytics project starts. Answer from the perspective of this stakeholder. Do not write
analytical questions or technical solutions for me. If information is not in the context, say clearly that it is an
assumption or the stakeholder's own view, and do not present it as a verified fact."
