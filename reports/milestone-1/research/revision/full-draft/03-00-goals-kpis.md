# 3. Business Goal, SMART Goal and KPIs

The themes of Section 2 lead to one goal for the project. Section 3.1 states the Business Goal, the outcome the
university wants, and explains where it comes from and which decisions it supports. Section 3.2 turns it into a SMART
Goal, one that is specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and time-bound, and states the assumptions on which its
figures rest. Section 3.3 sets the key performance indicators (KPIs), the measures that show progress towards the
SMART Goal.
