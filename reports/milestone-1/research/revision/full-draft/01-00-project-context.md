# 1. Project Context and Problem Statement

This proposal concerns students who do not complete their courses in a university's online and blended courses,
and in particular students who stop taking part partway through the semester. Section 1.1 describes the situation
the university faces, the Vietnamese rules that make non-completion a matter of institutional quality, and what
published studies report about how often non-completion happens, when it starts and what institutions do about it.
Section 1.2 then states the problem the project will address, the boundaries of the project, and the reasons why
data analytics is needed to address it.
