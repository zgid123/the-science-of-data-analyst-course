# 4. Analytical Question Ladder

The goal and KPIs of Section 3 are pursued through four analytical questions (AQ), one at each of the four levels of
analytics that the brief requires: descriptive, diagnostic, predictive and prescriptive [1]. Section 4.1 states the
questions, explains what each one adds and how it builds on the one before. Section 4.2 checks each question against
the brief's five quality criteria and shows two questions before and after refinement.
