# 2. Stakeholder Analysis and Requirement Findings

The problem stated in Section 1.2 rests on what five stakeholders said about it. Section 2.1 maps the stakeholders,
explains why each one matters to the problem and states the goals and decisions assumed for each before the
interviews. Section 2.2 describes how the interviews were prepared and carried out with generative artificial
intelligence (AI), and how their statements were recorded. Section 2.3 synthesises the answers into themes and
priorities, following the brief's steps of organising, identifying themes, prioritising and refining. Section 2.4
sets out where the stakeholders disagree, how the team resolved each disagreement, and what the stakeholders
confirmed, corrected and added when the team's understanding was taken back to them.

Throughout the proposal, a short code in parentheses, such as (PM1) or (VAD5), points to the stakeholder's own words.
The letters name the stakeholder (PM program manager, CL course lecturer, AD academic advisor, ST student, DO data
officer), a leading V marks a statement from the validation round, and the number orders the statements of one
stakeholder. Appendix II quotes every interview statement word for word, and Appendix III quotes every validation
statement.
