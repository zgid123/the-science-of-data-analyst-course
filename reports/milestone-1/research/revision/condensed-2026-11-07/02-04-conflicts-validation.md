## 2.4. Conflicts, Constraints and Validation

The stakeholders agree on the problem but differ on four points, resolved as Table 2.3 shows.

**Table 2.3. Conflicts between stakeholders and the team's resolution**

| Conflict | Statements | Resolution |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Early contact against false alarms and surveillance | PM3, CL6; CL8, ST7, AD9 | Two review points; factual reasons; staff check each name; students told at course start |
| Who makes contact | PM4, ST6, AD5 | Someone who knows the student; the support office takes harder cases |
| Program figures against student-level action | PM6; AD4, CL6 | Course and group patterns for the program; a short list for staff |
| Automation against unready data | DO9 | No automated action; data checked in the Milestone 2 audit |

In the validation round, the AI interviewer presented this understanding and a draft goal to each stakeholder and
asked what was right, what to correct and what was missing. Table 2.4 summarises the result (details in Appendix
III).

**Table 2.4. Validation results**

| Validation content | Result |
| :---- | :---- |
| Confirmed | Short, ordered list with a factual reason per name; contact by someone the student knows, never saying "at risk"; support-only use, with a person deciding; missed tasks as a better sign than LMS activity; the revised goal and target (VPM7) |
| Adjusted | Detection is late and patchy, not absent (VCL1, VAD1); review points in week 3 and week 5, outside registration and exams (VCL2, VAD2); completion means passing (VPM6); target over 2027–2028, baseline recounted under Circular 83/2026 (VPM5, VPM8); consent per purpose and legal approval (VDO2); family and employers never told (VST2) |
| Added | Program view of courses and groups (VPM3); first-year students (VPM4); a contact record (VCL4); students missing in several courses (VAD3); an "I'm fine" reply (VST3); a small pilot with comparison courses (VDO4, VPM9) |
| Not adopted, with reason | The advisor suggested measuring the project without a completion target (VAD4); the team keeps the target, because the brief asks for an organisational outcome, and reports it as progress rather than as the effect of contact. The lecturer suggested defining completion as "having done the work" (CL2); the team keeps passing, the outcome the program reports, and describes submitted work in Section 4.1. A student suggested a general email instead of personal contact (VST5); the team keeps personal contact, because generic emails are ignored (AD8), and adds a general notice to all students |
