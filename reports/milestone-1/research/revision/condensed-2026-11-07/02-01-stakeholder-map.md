## 2.1. Stakeholder Map

The brief requires the Program Manager, the Course Lecturer and the Academic Advisor / Student Support Officer [1],
who works in the student support office (the support office below). The map adds the Student, on whom every decision
acts, and the Learning Technology and Data Officer, whose office (the data office below) decides whether the data
needs of Section 6 can be met. Table 2.1 lists the goals and decisions assumed before the interviews, as hypotheses
to test.

**Table 2.1. Stakeholders, initial assumed goals and decisions possibly supported**

| Stakeholder | Role in the problem | Initial assumed goal | Decision possibly supported |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Program Manager (PM) | Owns completion figures; assigns support | More students complete | Where to put support; which courses to review |
| Course Lecturer (CL) | First to see who stops taking part | Students stay and pass | Whom to remind, and when |
| Academic Advisor / Student Support Officer (AD) | Contacts and supports students | Reach students early enough | Whom to contact first, with which support |
| Student (ST) | Subject of any data use and contact | Finish while working and caring for family | Whether to accept help or pause in time |
| Learning Technology and Data Officer (DO) | Runs the LMS and data extracts | Usable data without privacy breaches | What data to share, with whom, how often |

The interviews confirmed these hypotheses and sharpened two: the program manager's main decision is where to place
advisors' time within the semester (PM6), and the data officer's first decision is the purpose of data use, which
leadership and legal staff must approve (DO7).
