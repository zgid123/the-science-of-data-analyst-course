# S261 – Nguyễn Thị Linh (2025), Nâng cao hiệu quả công tác cố vấn học tập trong các trường đại học Việt Nam

## Summary in my own words

A 2025 review in the Vietnamese journal Tạp chí Thiết bị Giáo dục surveys the role and state of academic advising (cố vấn học tập, CVHT) at Vietnamese universities. Most universities have appointed advisors, but the effect falls short because of limits in resources, skills and management mechanisms. The author proposes better policy and procedures, more and better-trained advisors, IT tools for advising, and a monitoring and evaluation system. Only the abstract was readable; the PDF download timed out.
