# S253 – UM-Dearborn Early Warning Program (MASU innovation report, 2020)

## Summary in my own words

University of Michigan-Dearborn describes its EAB Navigate early-warning program. Faculty are asked at weeks 4 and 7 to mark at-risk students; alerts go to advisors and, from 2019, to tutoring and other support units. Faculty response rates were only 18–24%, heavy advisor caseloads delayed triage, alerts collided with registration, and decentralised advising produced different expectations. Students who met an advisor were retained at 93.9% versus 79.2% for those who did not, a self-selected comparison. The team names closing the loop with faculty as the key to the program.

## Key facts

- Faculty participation – "Faculty Response Rate: Fall 2018 - 18%"
- Timing – "Faculty are prompted to submit reports at week 4 and week 7 of the semester."
- Retention comparison – "Attended meeting with Advisor and Retained - 93.90%"
- Caseload pressure – "caseloads are sometimes quite heavy which creates problems"
- Registration clash – "receiving alerts on students during the same time as registration appointments"
- Closing the loop – "then close the loop with faculty once they meet with students"
