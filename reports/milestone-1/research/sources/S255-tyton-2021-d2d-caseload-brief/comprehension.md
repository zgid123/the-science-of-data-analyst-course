# S255 – Tyton Partners (2021), Driving Toward a Degree research brief #1: caseload

## Summary in my own words

Tyton Partners surveyed over 2,800 advisors, support staff and administrators at over 1,300 institutions in 2021. Advisors have named high caseloads among the top barriers to better advising in every survey year. Average primary-role caseloads are 292 (community colleges), 270 (four-year public) and 261 (four-year private). Mandatory advising does not scale with large caseloads, while proactive outreach sits at about 38% regardless of caseload; the brief warns about student contact fatigue. A regression links 100 fewer students per advisor with about one point higher retention, with acknowledged omitted-variable bias.
