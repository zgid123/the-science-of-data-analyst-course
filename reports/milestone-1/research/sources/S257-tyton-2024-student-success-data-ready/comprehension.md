# S257 – Tyton Partners (2024) press release, Driving Toward a Degree 2024

## Summary in my own words

Tyton's 2024 press release summarises surveys of over 3,000 administrators, advisors and students. Only 11% of administrators and advisors say their student success data is ready to seed generative AI models. Advisor turnover is rising: almost a third have been in role under two years, and a fifth of advisors with caseloads above 400 do not expect to stay five more years. Half of advisors have never used AI tools.

## Key facts

- Data readiness – "Only 11% of administrators and advisors say their student success data is ready to seed generative AI models."
- Tenure – "Nearly 1/3 of primary role advisors indicate that they have been in their role at their institution for less than two years."
- Attrition – "A fifth of primary-role advisors with caseload sizes of greater than 400 say they are unlikely to continue their role in the next five years."
- AI use – "49% of academic advisors and counselors having never used these tools"
