# S102 – Rahmani, Groot & Rahmani (2024), Dropout in online higher education: a systematic literature review

## Summary in my own words

Rahmani, Groot and Rahmani follow the PRISMA guidelines to review 110 articles published between 2013 and June 2023 on dropout in online higher education. They group risk factors into demographic, course-related, technology-related, motivational and support-related aspects. The main contributors they report are course quality, academic preparation, student satisfaction, learner motivation, system attributes and support services; students themselves report health, finances, technology problems, screen fatigue, isolation and workload as limits. The crawl holds the abstract and bibliographic record only, because the publisher's full text redirects to a login.

## Key facts

- Dropout in online higher education is a recognised concern – "a significant concern exists regarding dropout rates in online higher education (OHE)"
- Scope – "The selection process encompassed articles published between 2013 and June 2023, resulting in the inclusion of 110 relevant articles"
- Factor groups – "We examine demographic, course-related, technology-related, motivational, and support-related aspects that shape students' decisions in online learning programs."
- Main contributors – "The review highlights key contributors to dropout like the quality of the course, academic preparation, student satisfaction, learner motivation, system attributes, and support services."
- Limits reported by students – "health concerns, financial limitations, technological issues, screen fatigue, isolation, and academic workload, emerge as significant limitations reported by online learners"
