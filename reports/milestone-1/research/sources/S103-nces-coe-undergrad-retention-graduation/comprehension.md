# S103 – NCES Condition of Education: Undergraduate retention and graduation rates (May 2022)

## Summary in my own words

The US National Center for Education Statistics reports, from IPEDS data, how many first-time, full-time undergraduates return to the same institution the following fall (retention) and how many complete within 150% of normal time (graduation). For students who entered 4-year institutions in fall 2019, 82% were retained in fall 2020; at 2-year institutions the figure was 61%. Of the fall 2014 bachelor's cohort, 64% graduated from the same institution within six years. These are programme-level (degree) measures in a mostly campus-based system, not course-level completion in online courses; they show that about one in five first-year students at 4-year institutions does not return for the second year.

## Key facts

- Definition of retention – "Retention rates measure the percentage of first-time undergraduate students who return to the same institution the following fall."
- 4-year retention, fall 2019 entrants – "the overall retention rate in fall 2020 was 82 percent"
- 2-year retention, fall 2019 entrants – "At 2-year degree-granting institutions, the overall retention rate in fall 2020 for first-time, full-time degree-seeking undergraduate students who entered in fall 2019 was 61 percent."
- Six-year graduation, fall 2014 cohort – "Sixty-four percent of students who began seeking a bachelor’s degree at a 4-year institution in fall 2014 completed that degree at the same institution within 6 years."
- Date of the indicator – "Last Updated: May 2022"
