# S270 – Marks, Al-Ali & Rietsema (2016), LMS: a shift toward learning and academic analytics (iJET)

## Summary in my own words

The study combines a survey of 27 US IT directors and CIOs, ten interviews with IT directors and academic chairs or deans, and an Embry-Riddle case study. Administrators use LMS analytics for curriculum mapping, outcome measurement and accreditation. Manual processes had suffered inconsistencies, redundancies and data errors. Accountability and accreditation demands drive the push to collect institutional performance data.

## Key facts

- Method – "in-depth interviews with IT directors and academic administrators"
- Manual data problems – "inconsistencies, redundancies, data errors, labor-intensive process, and higher maintenance cost"
- Accreditation use – "helping prepare for accreditation self-studies, annual assessment planning, and program reviews"
- Interviewee – "our ability to automatically gather a large volume of data on actual students work and performance"
