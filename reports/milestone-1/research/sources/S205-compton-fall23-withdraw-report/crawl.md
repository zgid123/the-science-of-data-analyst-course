Fall 2023 Student Withdraw Report
January 2, 2024
The Office of Institutional Effectiveness distributed the Student Withdraw Survey to all students who withdrew from
a fall 2023 course.
• 92 students responded out of 1,469 students (1,907 enrollment withdrawals), which represents a 6.3%
response rate.
• 37% of respondents withdrew from two courses, and 8% withdrew from three courses. This is higher than
the actual withdrawal rates (23% withdrew from more than one course), indicating that students who
withdrew from more than one course are more likely to respond to the survey.
