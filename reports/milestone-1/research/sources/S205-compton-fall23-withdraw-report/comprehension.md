# S205 – Compton College (2024), Fall 2023 Student Withdraw Report

## Summary in my own words
A US community college surveyed all students who withdrew from a fall 2023 course (92 respondents of 1,469, 6.3% response) and publishes their open-ended answers verbatim. For the first withdrawn course, 58% agreed that personal or family responsibilities stopped them attending regularly and 55% did not feel prepared; only 20% had received academic support before withdrawing. Free-text answers name work, childcare, illness, depression, broken laptops, an asynchronous format, falling behind after a missed stretch, and an unresponsive professor. Many intend to retake the course later. These verbatim lines give the Student persona authentic phrasing.

## Key facts
- Response – "92 students responded out of 1,469 students (1,907 enrollment withdrawals), which represents a 6.3% response rate."
- Work and money – "I had to focus on work to help my family with money problems going on."
- Fell behind after illness – "i was falling behind so I decided to drop the class and pick it up later"
- Overload with job change – "There was an extensive amount of assignments and I lost a job and started a new job and couldn’t keep up."
- Tutoring did not help – "I couldn’t catch up. I was so lost when it came to the work."
- Asynchronous format – "I withdrew because of the challenge of asynchronous classes."
- New baby and job – "I could not keep up with the work I just had my son and my job got involved and interfered and I tried to caught up but it was completely too late."
- Childcare – "Struggling with time management having two kids with no daycare made it hard to complete classes"
- Mental health – "I was depressed"
- Unresponsive teacher – "Professor never answered any emails or communicated back with students for further assistance."
- Instructor-initiated withdrawal – "My English teacher had to withdraw me from class due to my lack of turning in my assignments on deadline."
- Positive instructor advice – "My instructor suggested I take the course for the last 8 weeks due to everything I had going on."
- Family responsibilities (course 1, n = 92): 41% strongly agree and 17% agree – "I had personal/family responsibilities that prevented me from regularly attending this course. 41% 17% 27% 14% 92"
- Little support used – "I received academic support and still decided to withdraw. 4% 16% 57% 23% 92"
