# S250 – Robbins (2013), Advisor load – NACADA Clearinghouse

## Summary in my own words

Robbins summarises caseload data from the 2011 NACADA National Survey of Academic Advising, edited by Carlstrom A. The median caseload of a full-time professional advisor is 296, and it rises with institution size. The article argues that no objective recommended caseload exists because advising duties and delivery modes (group, technology-supported, mandatory or voluntary advising) differ. It cites the CAS standard that caseloads must match the time needed for effective advising. The page was read through WebFetch, so only short verbatim excerpts are available.

## Key facts

- Median caseload – "the median case load of advisees per full-time professional academic advisor is 296"
- By size – "the median individual advisor case loads are 233, 333, and 600 advisees"
- No benchmark – "no objective recommended case load for advisors because of the factors discussed below"
- Carlstrom A. (Ed.) (NACADA Monograph No. 25, 2013) is the survey source – "NACADA National Survey of Academic Advising"
- Council for the Advancement of Standards (CAS standards, 2009) – "Academic advising caseloads must be consistent with the time required for the effective performance of this activity"
