# S256 – Mowreader (2023), Inside Higher Ed on Tyton's student support survey

## Summary in my own words

Inside Higher Ed reports the 2023 Tyton Driving Toward a Degree findings. Many students do not know which support services exist. Caseloads above 300 are linked to advisor burnout and less proactive contact with struggling students. Only 11% of advisers can easily see the demographics of their caseload, which points to weak data access. The article holds no direct quotes from advisers.

## Key facts

- Burnout – "overwhelming academic advisers with a larger caseload (over 300 students) can create burnout"
- Data access – "Only 11 percent of advisers are able to easily identify the demographics of their caseloads."
- Awareness – "60 percent of students were unaware of the full scope of services offered to them at their college or university,"
