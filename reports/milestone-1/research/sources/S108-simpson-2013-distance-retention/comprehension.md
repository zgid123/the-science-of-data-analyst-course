# S108 – Simpson (2013), Student retention in distance education: are we failing our students?

## Summary in my own words

Simpson compares graduation rates at a sample of distance-education institutions with those of conventional institutions and finds a "distance education deficit": many distance institutions graduate less than a quarter of the share of conventional ones. He examines the UK Open University in detail and argues that institutions focus on providing teaching materials, especially online, and too little on motivating students to learn. He reports accumulating evidence that proactive, motivational contact with students reduces dropout and can pay for itself.
