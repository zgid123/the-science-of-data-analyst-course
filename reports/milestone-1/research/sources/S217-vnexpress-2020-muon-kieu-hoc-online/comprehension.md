# S217 – VnExpress (2020), Students' many ways of attending online classes

## Summary in my own words
A VnExpress report from April 2020 (COVID-19 online teaching) follows Vietnamese students who attend Zoom classes while working or in poor connectivity. A final-year transport student joins from a mini-mart restroom during shifts, turns off camera and microphone, and goes back to work because the shop is short-staffed; a journalism student follows lectures from a reporting assignment with screen and microphone off and answers only when called, having used up his allowed absences; an agriculture student in rural Hà Giang sits on a school bench for wifi. The direct student quotes come from a WebFetch extract (short quotes, rest summarised), so the crawl is partial.

## Key facts
- Work during class – "Vì cửa hàng thiếu nhân viên nên em chưa thể xin nghỉ."
- Attendance rules drive log-ins – "em đã nghỉ đủ số buổi cho phép, nếu vắng mặt buổi này nữa sẽ bị cấm thi."
- Connectivity – "Do trường học đang sửa lại các phòng, em chỉ xin được ngồi ở ghế đá để bắt wifi."
- Remote students sleep at commune office for signal (district official) – "Có em ngủ luôn ở xã vì nhà xa mà về thì không có sóng điện thoại để học."
