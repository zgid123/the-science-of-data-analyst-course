# S101 – Lee & Choi (2011), A review of online course dropout research

## Summary in my own words

Lee and Choi's 2011 review in Educational Technology Research and Development is the most cited review of why students drop out of online courses in higher education. Only its bibliographic record could be read: the publisher page and the ResearchGate copy are blocked, and the Crossref, OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar records carry no abstract. Its content (dropout factors in three groups: student, course/program and environment, which interact) is therefore taken from Muljana and Luo (2019; S100), who summarise and quote it.
