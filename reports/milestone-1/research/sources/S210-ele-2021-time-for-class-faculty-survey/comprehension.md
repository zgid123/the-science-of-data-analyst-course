# S210 – Every Learner Everywhere (2022), Faculty survey on evidence-based teaching practices (Time for Class 2021)

## Summary in my own words
Every Learner Everywhere summarises Tyton Partners' Time for Class 2021 survey of more than 4,000 faculty and administrators at 1,600 US institutions. Faculty felt least ready to use data analytics and to build a sense of belonging; fewer than half saw data analytics as valuable, and adoption of data analytics stood at 32%. Tyton staff explain that faculty get product training from vendors but little institutional training on interpreting data, have limited time and do not always see value in the data. This grounds the Lecturer persona's ambivalence toward dashboards.

## Key facts
- Scale – "surveyed more than 4,000 faculty and administrators at 1,600 higher education institutions."
- Lowest readiness – "faculty felt the least readiness around data analytics and creating a sense of belonging and an inclusive learning environment."
- Value – "fewer than 50 percent perceived data analytics as valuable."
- Adoption – "bring data analytics up from 32 percent adoption"
- Training gap – "It’s easier for faculty to access training on how to use the interface and the features of the product than to get training on how to look at data"
- Time and value – "they have limited time and don’t always see value in the data."
- Without data, faculty rely on qualitative cues – "then you’re relying primarily on qualitative indicators from students,"
