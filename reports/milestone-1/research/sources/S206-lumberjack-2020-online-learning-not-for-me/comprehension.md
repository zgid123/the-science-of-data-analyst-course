# S206 – Parra (2020), The Lumberjack: Online learning isn't for me

## Summary in my own words
A final-year Humboldt State University student writes in the student newspaper that the emergency move online in spring 2020 drained her motivation: online classes "don't feel like real learning", home is full of distractions, and Zoom sessions are awkward. She values online courses for fitting around work when they have a fixed weekly rhythm (posts Monday, comments Wednesday, quiz Friday). A reader comment from a first-year student describes doubting her own intelligence after a fully virtual first term. This gives the Student persona first-person phrasing about lost motivation and self-doubt.

## Key facts
- Lost motivation – "We’ve all lost motivation, and quite frankly, it doesn’t even feel as if we’re in school anymore."
- Home distractions – "There are too many distractions at home that I can’t get away from that just make it even harder to learn."
- Online fits around work – "online classes are nice to clear up some room in your schedule for work or other classes."
- Predictable weekly rhythm – "discussion posts due Monday, comments due Wednesday and a quiz every Friday"
- Teacher readiness matters – "when your professors start to panic, you start to panic."
- Self-doubt (reader comment) – "I was beginning to doubt my intelligence, I thought maybe they had let me in to university accidentally!"
- Boring online lectures (reader comment) – "Some of the lectures are intensely boring online and as you say, it’s so difficult to stay motivated."
