# S208 – Nguyễn Q. A. & Nguyễn T. H. A. (2023), Causes of dropout among online distance students at Hanoi Open University

## Summary in my own words
A Vietnamese survey of students who dropped out of Hanoi Open University's (EHOU) online distance programmes between 2020 and 2023 (480 responses, PLS model) groups dropout factors into student factors (age, health, time management, family, work and finances), institutional factors (programme and lecturers, technology infrastructure, learning materials, academic advising and student support, assessment) and social-environment factors (negative social perception of distance learning, feedback from earlier learners, competition). Academic advising and student support had the largest effect on dropout, followed by programme and lecturers and technology infrastructure. Only the abstract is on disk.
