# Reader test – Vietnamese main text (reference.md §4.3, stage 5)

Reader: Vietnamese lecturer of data analytics marking a student proposal, reading only the `.vi.md` files (main text
01-00 to 09-00, plus the abbreviation page 00-1 and the glossary 00-2). Evidence codes and bracketed citations ignored.

Overall judgement: yes, a reader in the field could explain the document afterwards. The argument (students who
disengage are found late; build a short, reasoned contact list at weeks 3 and 5; measure pass rate on pilot courses;
four analytical questions from descriptive to prescriptive; data and privacy limits; three decisions) comes through on
a single read, and most sentences are short, grammatical Vietnamese. The difficulties are concentrated in a few places:
(a) Table 2.4's "Không áp dụng" row and the second paragraph of Section 8, which argue against alternatives the reader
never sees; (b) the SMART goal timeline and baseline wording, where "mức cơ sở tính lại", "mức cơ sở giả định", "thời
hạn một năm" and "so với hai năm học trước đó" do not add up without rereading; (c) a layer of coined or calqued terms
("khả năng liên hệ của cán bộ", "ngừng tham gia học tập", "lượt đăng ký học phần", "AI phỏng vấn", "persona", "họ kỹ
thuật", "phân tích sống còn", "kiểm định theo thời gian") that a Vietnamese colleague would not use; and (d) "cán bộ"
and "chương trình đào tạo" used as generic actors, so I often had to guess who exactly acts. The text reads as a careful
translation rather than as Vietnamese written from scratch, but it is followable.
