# Reader test 3: Vietnamese twins of the Milestone 1 report

Overall judgement: yes, a lecturer in the field could explain this document after one reading. The problem, the five stakeholders, the goal, the KPIs, the four questions and the privacy limits all come through, and most sentences read as ordinary academic Vietnamese. The document is not yet effortless, though, and the difficulty sits in three places. First, there are real gaps in meaning, not only in wording: the school is declared hypothetical in Section 1.1, yet Sections 3.2 and 8 talk about its real records and its real data; the timeline of the SMART Goal (pilot in one semester, target judged a year later) does not say what happens in between; and the figure of 1,200 students does not fit the "small pilot" of Section 6.2. Second, one awkward noun phrase carries the argument of the whole report, "số sinh viên (mà) cán bộ liên hệ được mỗi tuần", and it makes me stop each time it appears, because the head noun is hard to find and no short name exists for the idea. Third, the tables in Section 2 are telegraphic: cells such as those in Bảng 2.4 list six compressed results with no verbs, so I knew the words but not what had changed. The rest is small-scale translated phrasing ("nhận ra", "bỏ lỡ bài", "người sinh viên quen biết") that I can read through but that tells me a translation sits underneath. Section 8 and Section 6.1 needed the most rereading for their length; Section 3.1 was the cleanest.
