# 2. AQ-to-Technical-Task Mapping

With the entry gate met (Section 1.2), each question is first described by what it needs and only then translated into
a technical task, so the method follows from the question rather than from habit or a suggestion of artificial intelligence (AI) [1]. Section
2.1 gives the AQ Modeling Briefs, and Section 2.2 the technical task that each brief implies.
