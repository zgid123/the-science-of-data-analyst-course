# 3. Candidate Modeling Approaches

With the technical task of each question fixed in Part 2, each question receives its methods. The brief asks for at least one reasonable baseline per
question and, where modeling is needed, two or three candidate families before a primary approach is chosen, and it
rejects an algorithm's name or popularity as a reason [1]. Section 3.1 sets out the selection criteria and the
Candidate Solution Matrix, Section 3.2 the descriptive and diagnostic questions, Section 3.3 the predictive question
and Section 3.4 the ranking under capacity.
