# Appendices

The appendices hold the full design behind the main text: the AQ Modeling Briefs (Appendix I), the candidate
comparison against the eight criteria (Appendix II), the Evaluation Design Cards and metric definitions (Appendix
III), the validation and test protocol with sample sizes (Appendix IV), the error and fairness analysis (Appendix V),
the risk register (Appendix VI), the AI Decision Log with the reviewers' prompts (Appendix VII) and the full
traceability with Change Log entry C14 (Appendix VIII). Counts come from the prepared views of
OULAD-N04-prepared-1.0; outcome counts use the 2013 training runs only.
