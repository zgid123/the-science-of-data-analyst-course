# 1. Project Context and Continuity

Milestone 4 turns the analytical questions and the analytic-ready dataset of Milestone 3 into a reasoned plan for
modeling and evaluation [1]. The proposal covers Level 1 of the brief: for each question it fixes the technical
task, a baseline and candidate methods, the chosen approach with its reasons, the evaluation and the risks, before any
model is trained. A team that completes Level 1 well completes the milestone, and the optional prototype of Level 2
cannot replace a weak strategy [1]; Part 7 therefore specifies the prototype as a protocol, and no model
performance is stated anywhere. Section 1.1 restates the goal, the decisions and the questions, and Section 1.2 the
prepared data and the entry gate.
