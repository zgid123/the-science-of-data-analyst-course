## 1.1. Goal, Decisions and Analytical Questions

The Business Goal is to improve successful course completion in a university programme's online and blended courses
by identifying students who stop taking part early enough to support them within the semester. The specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and time-bound (SMART) Goal raises
the completion rate of students enrolled after the add-drop period from an assumed 70% to 75% by the end of
2027–2028, and that rate is the primary key performance indicator (KPI). Secondary KPIs are the shares of students with early signs contacted by week 5 and of listed students contacted within seven days. The proposal supports three decisions: who is listed for contact (D1), where advisors'
time goes (D2) and whether to extend after the pilot (D3).

The data are the Open University Learning Analytics Dataset (OULAD): 22 runs of seven Open University (OU) modules
from 2013 and 2014 [2], prepared in Milestone 3 as enrolments, each one student in one module run. Only analytical questions (AQ)
that remain valid after the AQ–Data Fit of Milestone 3 may be modelled, and a deferred part may not return without a
recorded change [1]. Table 1.1 gives the four AQs as they stand after Change Log entries
C01–C13 of Milestones 2 and 3, with their Milestone 3 readiness and the decision each supports.

**Table 1.1. AQs entering Milestone 4**

| AQ | Analytics level | Question | Readiness after Milestone 3 | Decision |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| AQ1 | Descriptive | Pass, fail and withdrawal shares by module, run, start month and year; week of the first missed task | Ready | D2, D3 |
| AQ2 | Diagnostic | Signs by the end of week 4 that go with later non-completion, within each module | Ready (association) | D2, reasons on D1 |
| AQ3 | Predictive | How well non-completion is estimated by the end of weeks 2 and 4, against an early-sign rule, on a later run | Ready | D1 |
| AQ4 | Prescriptive (ranking) | Which enrolments rank first under an assumed capacity, with which gain over the run's base rate and recall, overall and by deprivation band and disability | Revised: ranking only, choice of contact deferred | D1 |

The early-sign rule of AQ3 flags a student who, by the cut-off, has not submitted a task that was due, has submitted a
tutor-marked assignment (TMA) late, or has had no activity on the learning platform in the previous seven days (Change Log entry C10).
List quality in AQ4 is measured as the gain over each run's base rate and as recall against the most a list of that
length can reach (Change Log entry C13). The Business Goal, the SMART Goal and the three decisions are unchanged.
