# S514 – Classification

## Summary in my own words

Barocas, Hardt and Narayanan's textbook chapter on classification defines three group fairness criteria, independence, separation and sufficiency, and shows that imposing any two of them at once leaves only degenerate solutions in general, so observing one criterion implies others are violated.

## Key facts

- Criteria named – "Independence"
- Any two criteria conflict – "We should therefore suspect that imposing any two of them simultaneously over-constrains the space to the point where only degenerate solutions remain."
- Consequence – "What this shows, in particular, is that if we observe that one criterion holds, we expect others to be violated."
