# S522 – The Open University (2025). Responsible AI Policy, version 1.3 (approved by the University

## Summary in my own words

The Open University's Responsible AI Policy (version 1.3, approved October 2025, published January 2026, review date November 2026) replaces its Data Ethics Policy, which itself replaced the 2014 learning-analytics ethics policy. It requires that AI decisions do not replace or diminish humans in critical decisions, that AI systems be overseen by humans through supervision, monitoring and accountability, and that AI not perpetuate or amplify biases.
