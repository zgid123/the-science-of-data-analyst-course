# S204 – It's about Time: Discrete-Time Survival Analysis (Singer & Willett, Journal of Educational Statistics 1993)

## Summary in my own words

Singer and Willett show educational researchers how to study whether and when events happen using discrete-time survival analysis. They fit a discrete-time hazard model by maximum likelihood with ordinary logistic regression software on person-period data, and they explain the kinds of main effects and interactions (including with time) the model can carry. Only the abstract and bibliographic record were readable (publisher closed).

## Key facts

- Question form – "Educational researchers frequently ask whether and, if so, when events occur."
- Method fits with logistic regression – "we derive maximum likelihood estimators for the parameters of a discrete-time hazard model, and we show how the model can befit using standard logistic regression software."
- Framework claim – "the methods of discrete-time survival analysis provide educational statisticians with an ideal framework for studying event occurrence."
- Example size – "longitudinal data on the career paths of 3,941 special educators"
