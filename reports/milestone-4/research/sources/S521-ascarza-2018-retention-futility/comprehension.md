# S521 – Retention Futility: Targeting High-Risk Customers Might be Ineffective

## Summary in my own words

Ascarza combines two field experiments with machine learning to show that the customers at the highest predicted risk of churning are not necessarily those who respond best to a retention intervention. She recommends targeting by sensitivity to the intervention (lift), estimated from randomised trials, rather than by risk. Only the abstract (OpenAlex metadata) was read.

## Key facts

- Highest risk is not the best target – "the author demonstrates that customers identified as having the highest risk of churning are not necessarily the best targets for proactive churn programs."
- Target by response to intervention – "target customers on the basis of their sensitivity to the intervention, regardless of their risk of churning."
- Use randomised trials to estimate heterogeneity – "the author encourages firms and researchers using randomized trials (or A/B tests) to look beyond the average effect of interventions"
