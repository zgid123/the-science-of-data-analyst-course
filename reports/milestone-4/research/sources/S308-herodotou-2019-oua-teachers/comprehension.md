# S308 – A Large-Scale Implementation of Predictive Learning Analytics: The Teachers' Role and Perspective (Herodotou et al., ETR&D 2019)

## Summary in my own words

The OU team tested whether giving teachers OU Analyse predictions helps them identify and support at-risk students, in a university-wide study with 59 teachers, nine courses and 1,325 students. Teachers who engaged with the predictions positively affected student performance, and interviews showed how they used the data in their teaching and interventions. Only the abstract was readable.

## Key facts

- PLA purpose – "predictive learning analytics (PLA) are used to identify learners who may not complete a course, typically described as being at risk."
- Study scale – "a university-wide, multi-methods study with 59 teachers, nine courses, and 1325 students"
- Finding (author-reported) – "teachers can positively affect students’ performance when engaged with PLA."
- Mixed uptake – "Mixed effects are observed as to how teachers perceive, use, and interpret PLA data"

## Against reference.md

Confirms the outline's framing that the prediction serves tutors who decide whom to contact (D-level decision support, human in the loop). The positive effect is associational at teacher level and does not establish that contact causes completion; consistent with the outline's "contact deferred".
