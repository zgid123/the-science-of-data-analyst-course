# S504 – NIST (2023). Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0), NIST AI 100-1

## Summary in my own words

NIST's AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 is a voluntary, sector-agnostic framework organised in four functions: Govern, Map, Measure and Manage. It treats validity and reliability as the base of trustworthiness and says deployed systems are assessed by ongoing testing or monitoring, with human intervention where the system cannot detect or correct its errors.

## Key facts

- Four functions – "govern, map, measure, and manage AI risks."
- Voluntary and use-case agnostic – "voluntary, rights-preserving, non-sector-specific, and use-case agnostic"
- Ongoing monitoring – "Validity and reliability for deployed AI systems are often assessed by ongoing testing or"
- Human intervention – "may need to include human intervention in cases where the AI system cannot detect or correct errors."
- Version – "NIST AI 100-1"
