# S505 – Model Cards for Model Reporting

## Summary in my own words

Mitchell et al. propose model cards: short documents released with a trained model that report intended uses and out-of-scope uses, the factors for which performance may vary, and performance disaggregated by groups, including intersectional ones. They argue this is especially needed in domains such as education.

## Key facts

- Proposal – "we recommend that released models be accompanied by documentation detailing their performance characteristics."
- Domains including education – "tasks in areas such as law enforcement, medicine, education, and employment."
- Disaggregated evaluation – "Disaggregated by unitary and intersectional groups such as cultural, demographic, or phenotypic population groups."
- Gebru T. et al. (CACM 2021; S506) complement – "Where Datasheets highlight characteristics of the data feeding into the model"
