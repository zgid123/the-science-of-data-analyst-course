# Appendices

The appendices hold the detail behind the main text: the four AQ Modeling Briefs in full, the predictors of AQ3 and the
candidate comparison against the eight criteria (Appendix I), the four Evaluation Design Cards in full and the metric
definitions (Appendix II), the validation and test protocol with sample sizes and the prototype protocol (Appendix III),
the error and fairness analysis (Appendix IV), the risk register with the full risk notes (Appendix V), the literature
searches of the AI Decision Log, the decisions on the AI critiques and the reviewers' prompts (Appendix VI), and the
secondary KPIs, the earlier changes, the full traceability and Change Log entry C14 (Appendix VII). Counts come from the
prepared views of OULAD-N04-prepared-1.0; outcome counts use the 2013 training runs only.
