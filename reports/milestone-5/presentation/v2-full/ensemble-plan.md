# Ensemble plan – full Milestone 5 deck (no slide limit)

Inputs: the three full drafts in `drafts/` (A Storytelling with Data, 70 slides; B SCQA and pyramid, 76; C
data-storytelling arc, 89), written from `drafts/BRIEF.md`. This plan takes the union of their topics in story
order, one idea per slide, and names the draft slides each ensembled slide draws on (A12 = draft A slide 12). The
`Line` column keeps the drafts' main / detail call where they agree and decides where they differ; it is the input to
the appendix proposal, which is made only after the full deck is built and read.

Ensembling rules:
- Lead and on-slide content: take the clearest of the source slides; combine only facts that serve the slide's one
  idea. Speaker notes: rewrite from the sources so each note opens from the previous slide in this plan and hands over
  to the next; 90–140 words.
- The deck already built (30 slides) is reused where a slide here matches one there (column `Deck`): its on-slide
  wording was checked for stand-alone reading and Vietnamese fluency, so keep it unless a source draft adds a fact.
- Numbers: the drafts flagged population pairs that must stay named: the run range 24.9–58.3% (all 22 runs) against
  24.9–57.5% (2013 runs); 1,586 repeat students at day 27 against 1,962 over all 2014 enrolments; missing exam results
  as "18 of 24 exams" (Table 6.2) and "2 of 7 modules" (§5.1). Never put the two members of a pair on one slide
  without their populations. The completion range 45.7–73.8% (App VI) uses a different population from the 26.3%
  non-completion of AAA (§11.2); leave it out.
- No reference numbers in brackets on slides: name the source in words ("a published Open University study
  (Herodotou et al., 2019)"); bracketed numbers stay in the notes' Sources line only.
- Dataset table and column names (studentVle, final_result) appear only with their plain meaning beside them.
