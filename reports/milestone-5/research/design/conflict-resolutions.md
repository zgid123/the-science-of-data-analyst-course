# Conflict resolutions between Milestones 1–4 (design file, T09)

One row per conflict listed in the T05, T06 and T08 handoff notes of `PROGRESS.md`, plus two found while checking
them (R15, R16). Each conflict was checked in the local crawls of the milestone reports (S101–S106 `crawl.md`)
before it was resolved; the crawl line numbers are given where no claim ID carries the passage. "New Change Log
entry" says whether the resolution changes how a question is measured without an existing entry; such entries are
drafted in `change-log-m5.md`.

Outcome: 16 conflicts; 13 resolved from recorded evidence without a new entry; 3 need a new entry: C15 (R14, a
Milestone 5 change), C16 (R3, an unrecorded change made in Milestones 3–4) and C17 (R2, an ambiguity left open by
C06 and C10; proposed, for the team to confirm).
