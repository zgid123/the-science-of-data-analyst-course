# AQ-by-AQ answers (design file, T09)

Working design file for report Section 11 (brief section 5). Columns as in the brief. AQ1 and AQ2 carry the real
results on the 9 runs of 2013; AQ3 and AQ4 carry expected outputs in the proposal tense, with the success criteria
fixed in Milestone 4 and no metric value. Interpretation rules of the brief applied: descriptive = what happened;
diagnostic = association, not cause; predictive = ability to predict on a proper validation, not cause;
prescriptive on observational data = a decision option to test, not a proven intervention.
