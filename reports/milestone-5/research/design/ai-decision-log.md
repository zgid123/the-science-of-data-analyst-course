# AI Decision Log, Milestones 1–5 (design file, T09)

Working design file for report Section 13 and the AI Decision Log of the Final Project Package (brief sections 6,
6.1, 7). Columns as in brief 6.1. The rows "M1 / M5" to "M4 / M5" summarise the AI use of each milestone and how
Milestone 5 carries its result into the final report; the rows "M5 – …" record the AI use of Milestone 5 itself. Rows
marked PLACEHOLDER are completed by the task named in them; their cells stay empty until then.
