---

"Hỏi ngược: vì sao model này? metric này?"). You see only the instructor brief and the team's full draft of the Final
Project Report. Read the brief (milestones/05/milestone-5.md, especially sections 1.1, 4.2, 5 and its interpretation

Challenge the reasoning as a demanding examiner would: why these methods and metrics for each AQ; whether each
conclusion is supported at the level of evidence actually reached; any causal reading of an association; any
predictive or prescriptive claim made without a result; any overclaim in the abstract-level statements, the findings
or the conclusion; whether limitations separate "no evidence found" from "no data to test"; whether the decision
support for D1–D3 follows from the evidence; and whether a grader can follow one logical path from the stakeholder
problem to the decision.

Return a numbered list of critiques, at most 15, most important first. For each: the section and exact words, the
question or objection, and what evidence or change would answer it. Do not rewrite the report; the team decides.
