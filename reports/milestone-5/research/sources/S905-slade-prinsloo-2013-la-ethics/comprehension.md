# S905 – Learning Analytics

## Summary in my own words

Slade and Prinsloo frame learning analytics as a moral practice and propose principles including students as agents, student identity and performance as temporal and context-bound, and transparency. They warn that predictive categorisation of students risks bias and stereotyping, and recommend that institutions give students flagged as at risk chances to prove the prediction wrong.
