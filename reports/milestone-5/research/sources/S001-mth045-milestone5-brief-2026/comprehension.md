# S001 – MTH045 – The Science of Data Analytics. (2026). Milestone P5: Final integration & project report [Course brief]

## Summary in my own words

The instructor brief for the last milestone. It asks each team to integrate selected results of Milestones 1–4 into
one Final Project Report that a grader can follow from the stakeholder problem to the final analytical questions,
the real data, the preparation, the methods, the evaluation and back to the decision and its educational value,
while seeing clearly what the project cannot yet conclude. It fixes the report structure (Abstract and Sections
1–14, each with its source milestone, aim, content, required evidence and a self-check question), two mandatory
integration tables (the Final Project Traceability Matrix and the AQ Revision Log), the AQ-by-AQ answer table with
interpretation rules per analytics level, the rules for writing a Level-1-only project, the place of Generative AI
and a minimum AI Decision Log, the Final Project Package, a 100-point rubric and a submission checklist.
