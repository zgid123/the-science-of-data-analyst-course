# S908 – Learning Analytics Should Not Promote One Size Fits All (Gašević, Dawson, Rogers & Gašević, Internet and Higher Education 2016)

## Summary in my own words

The study examined how instructional conditions (course design, how each course uses the learning management system) shape the prediction of academic success from trace data across several courses. Its title states the conclusion: predictive models should not assume one general model fits all courses. Only the bibliographic record was readable; the content of the conclusion is known from the title and from its role in the field, and the report should not quote numbers from it.
