# S906 – Algorithmic Bias in Education

## Summary in my own words

Baker and Hawn review algorithmic bias in education, showing that bias has been found across many groups and that much research does not report learner demographics. Their key practical point is that group membership must be recorded to audit a model, and they recommend collecting demographic data. They summarise the impossibility results of Kleinberg et al. and Chouldechova.

## Key facts

- No audit without group data – "If we do not collect data on group membership, we cannot analyze our data sets"
- Recommendation – "we recommend that researchers collect extensive demographic data on learners whenever possible."
- Kleinberg J. et al. (ITCS 2017; S508) summarised – "describe how the fairness criteria of calibration, balance for the positive class, and balance in the negative class cannot be simultaneously satisfied, except in special cases of perfect prediction and equal base rates"
