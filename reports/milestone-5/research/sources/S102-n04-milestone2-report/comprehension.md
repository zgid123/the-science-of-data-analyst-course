# S102 – N04 (2026). Milestone 2 report: data requirements, dataset selection and acquisition

## Summary in my own words

The Milestone 2 report freezes the Milestone 1 baseline with checksums, turns AQ1–AQ4 into a data requirement matrix with six must-have gates (G1–G5 with G2 in two parts), searches open repositories and scores seven candidates on the brief's ten criteria. OULAD is selected as the only candidate passing the timing and licence gates with a dated withdrawal, conditionally on three facts that the structural verification then checks. It documents acquisition from the figshare deposit (version 1, CC BY 4.0, accessed 6 November 2026), the structure of the seven tables, and maps each AQ to real variables with Partial coverage. Eight controlled revisions C01–C08 adapt the questions to OULAD without changing the Business Goal, SMART Goal or decisions.
