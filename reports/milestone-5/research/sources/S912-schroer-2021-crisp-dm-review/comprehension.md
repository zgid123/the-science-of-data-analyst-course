# S912 – Schröer, Kruse & Gómez (2021). A systematic literature review on applying CRISP-DM process model

## Summary in my own words

A systematic review of data-mining studies in IEEE, ScienceDirect and ACM that applied CRISP-DM, twenty years after its release in 2000. It finds CRISP-DM is still the de-facto, industry-independent standard process model with six phases, but most studies do not plan a deployment phase. Only the abstract and bibliographic record could be read (ScienceDirect returned HTTP 403). For our report it names the process model that the five milestones follow (business understanding to evaluation) and supports stating that deployment is outside the project.
