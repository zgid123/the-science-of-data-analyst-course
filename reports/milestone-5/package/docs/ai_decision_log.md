# AI Decision Log, Milestones 1–5

This document reproduces the AI Decision Log of the final report: Section 13 (Generative AI Use and Reflection) and
its full table in Appendix XVI (Table A.43), in the columns of the course brief. Section and appendix numbers below
refer to the final report; Change Log entries (C13, C15–C17) are in `data/metadata/change_log.md`.

We used generative AI as a simulated stakeholder, a research and code assistant, a critic and a writing partner, and in
every role under one rule: AI was never a source of data, statistics, licence information or results, and every fact it
offered was checked against a saved source, the data files or code that we ran. The rows "M1 / M5" to "M4 / M5"
summarise the AI use of each earlier milestone and how its result is carried into the final report; the rows for
Milestone 5 record the AI use in the integration itself. The last row covers the drafting and review of the full
report. No AI produced a metric value, threshold or result.

In Milestone 5, AI agents extracted 369 statements, each checked automatically against a quotation from its source;
wrote the AQ1 and AQ2 code, whose results we accepted only after the automated consistency checks passed, a byte-identical rerun and an
independent recomputation; and reviewed the full draft, whose 66 critiques we checked against the data: 42 accepted, 14
in part, 10 handled in the finishing steps and none rejected (Section 13.1). The decisions stayed ours: to keep the 2014
runs closed, to keep one early-sign rule, to report AQ3 and AQ4 as designs and to state every association as an
association (Section 13.2).
