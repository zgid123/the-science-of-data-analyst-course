# Controlled Change Log, entries C01–C17

Every change to the questions, KPIs, populations or decisions from the first proposal to the final report, with the
content before and after the change, its reason in the data and its effect on the questions (AQ1–AQ4), the KPIs or
the decisions (D1–D3). C01–C08 come from the data search of Milestone 2, C09–C13 from the data understanding of
Milestone 3, C14 from the evaluation design of Milestone 4 and C15–C17 from the final integration in Milestone 5. The
data-quality issues named in the Reason column (DQ01–DQ19) are in `quality_issue_log.md`.
