## 12.1. Future Work and Data Needed

One step runs on OULAD as it stands: a later review point for the non-completers who still look active at week 4,
whom the AQ3 lists rarely reach, designed and fixed before it is tested (Table A.42, Appendix XV). At the university, the baseline is recounted under the add-drop rule and the assumed
values are replaced by its records, and the list first needs the risk classification that Vietnamese law requires for
ranking learners (Appendix XV). A randomised pilot among the flagged students of the pilot courses, with time zero at the eligibility cut-off, then
tests whether contact helps, because only random assignment separates the effect of contact from the reasons students
are flagged [14]; its contacts are logged with their outcomes before any model is retrained, since a model retrained
on outcomes shaped by contact would learn its own influence.
