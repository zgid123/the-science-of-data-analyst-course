## 4.1. Dataset Selection, Provenance and Licence

In Milestone 2 we searched data repositories, among them the University of California, Irvine (UCI) Machine Learning
Repository, with keywords from the requirements and screened seven candidates, scoring each
from 0 to 2 on the brief's ten criteria, with the gates deciding first (Table 4.2; the scores are in Table A.10,
Appendix IV). OULAD is the only candidate that records a dated withdrawal, dated submissions and daily clicks, so it was
chosen for its fit with the questions, not for ease of access; its selection was conditional on the files, and the
structural check confirmed G1 and G5 and met G3 for five of the seven modules. Our copy is version 1 of the figshare
deposit (doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.5081998.v1), accessed on 6 November 2026 with pinned SHA-256 (Secure Hash Algorithm, 256-bit) checksums (Table A.11).
It is described in the data descriptor of Kuzilek, Hlosta and Zdrahal, released anonymised under a CC BY 4.0 licence
[5], and we make no attempt at re-identification.
