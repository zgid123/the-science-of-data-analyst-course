# 10. Experimental Implementation and Results

The project completed Level 1, the compulsory level: the modeling strategy of Section 8 and the evaluation design of
Section 9. The optional Level 2 prototype for AQ3 and AQ4 was specified in full in Milestone 4 and has not been run, so
no model metric, chart or confusion matrix exists. This is our choice of scope: Level 2 is optional, and the 13 runs of
2014 are the only independent test, which we keep for one complete run of the frozen protocol. The AQ1 and AQ2
analyses of Section 11 are descriptive and diagnostic analyses, not this prototype: they train no predictive model, and
although they describe the 2014 runs, they change nothing in the AQ3 design that Milestone 4 fixed on 2013 alone (C15).
The protocol fixes three baselines and four candidate configurations in advance (Table A.25, Appendix X).
