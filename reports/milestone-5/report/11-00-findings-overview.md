# 11. Findings, Discussion and Decision Support

AQ1 and AQ2 needed no model training, so we ran them as Milestone 4 had fixed them on the 9 module runs of 2013 and
again on the 13 runs of 2014; AQ3 and AQ4 are answered with their expected outputs. Each answer is read by its
level: the AQ2 estimates describe associations, not causes [10]; a predictive claim needs evaluation on unseen data
[10]; and a recommendation from observational data is an option to test, since questions of effectiveness are ideally
answered by a randomised experiment [14]. Every finding is an effect size with its 95% interval and population, and
Table A.26 (Appendix XI) states what each answer can and cannot state. Confidence is High when two independent
computations verify the counts on the stated population, which says nothing about the university's courses; Moderate
when estimates keep their direction under the checks but rest on observational data or a model with a known weakness; and Not
applicable when no result exists yet.
