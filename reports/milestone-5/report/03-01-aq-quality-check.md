## 3.1. Quality Check of the Final Questions

The brief requires every question to be clear, specific, scoped, data-oriented and answerable. A question is clear when
it asks one thing a reader can restate, specific when it names its measure and groups, scoped when it fixes its
population and period, data-oriented when it names its data, and answerable when those data can settle it. The quality
check column of Table 3.1 gives the result, and Table A.4 (Appendix III) gives the reason for each criterion and
question. All four questions are clear, specific, scoped and data-oriented in their final wording. Answerability
differs: AQ1 and AQ2 are answered for the 2013 and 2014 runs, AQ3 is answerable by a design not yet run, and only the ranking
part of AQ4 is answerable, because no data record contacts, which is why its contact part is deferred rather than
answered with assumptions.
