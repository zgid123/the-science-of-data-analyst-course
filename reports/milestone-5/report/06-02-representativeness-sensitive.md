## 6.2. Temporal Coverage, Representativeness and Sensitive Fields

Weekly clicks peak in week 3, so the cut-offs at day 13 and day 27 fall in the weeks of highest activity, and because
each module has at most one earlier run of the same start month, the only test of a model on a later run is to train
on 2013 and test on 2014. OULAD represents Open University modules chosen partly because many students failed them, so
results must be checked on the university's records before they inform D1 to D3, groups are compared within module,
and module CCC appears only in the test year. Six sensitive attributes, gender, age band, disability, deprivation band,
region and highest education, serve the audit and stay outside the predictors, except highest education (Table A.16,
Appendix VI). They are kept because bias cannot be audited without data on group membership [6], and a fairness
study on OULAD reproduced a disparity of five to six percentage points in favour of male students [7]. Under
Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Law, re-identifying de-identified data is prohibited [8], so OULAD is never linked
to another source.
