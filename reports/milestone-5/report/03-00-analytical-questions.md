# 3. Analytical Questions and Analytics Levels

Four analytical questions (AQ), AQ1–AQ4, supply the evidence the three decisions need, one per analytics level, all on one unit, a
student's enrolment in one module run. Each answer is read by its level: a descriptive answer states what happened, a
diagnostic answer an association, a predictive answer how well an outcome is foreseen on proper validation, and a
prescriptive answer on observational data an option to test. Table 3.1 gives the final questions with the result of
their quality check (Section 3.1); on OULAD a semester becomes a module run, and the data cut-offs of the week-3 and
week-5 lists fall on day 13 and day 27.

**Table 3.1. Final questions AQ1–AQ4**

| AQ | Level | Final question | Scope | Decision | Quality check | Status |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| AQ1 | Descriptive | What share of enrolments end in pass, fail or withdrawal, by module, run, start month and year, and in which week non-completers first miss an assessed task? | Registered at day 14; 22 runs of 2013–2014 | D2, D3 | All five met | Answered |
| AQ2 | Diagnostic (association only) | Which signs recorded by the end of week 4 (missed and late tasks, inactivity, activity against the run's median) go with later non-completion, compared within each module? | Registered at day 27; 2013, replicated on 2014 | D2; reasons in D1 | All five met; association only | Answered |
| AQ3 | Predictive | How well can non-completion be estimated from the data up to the end of week 2 and of week 4, compared with the early-sign rule, when a model built on the 2013 runs is applied to the 2014 runs? | Days 13 and 27; test on the 13 runs of 2014 | D1 | Answered by a single test on 2014 | Answered |
| AQ4 | Prescriptive (ranking under capacity) | Which enrolments should rank first on a list of an assumed capacity, with what gain over a random list and what recall, overall and by deprivation band and disability? | 2014 test runs; 3 or 5 per 20 students | D1 | Ranking answerable; contact has no data | Partial |

The five criteria judge a question's wording; its status says how much results answer: Answered when they answer all of it, Partial when they answer its core while a named part waits,
Deferred when its design is complete but no result exists, and Dropped when it is abandoned; no question is dropped. A
list is judged by normalised recall, the share of later non-completers it reaches against the most a list of its length
can reach, and by lift over the run's base rate (C13).
