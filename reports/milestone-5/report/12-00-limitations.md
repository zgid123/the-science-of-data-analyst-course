# 12. Limitations and Future Work

We keep two situations apart: "no evidence found" when a comparison was made and showed no clear difference, and "no
data to test" when OULAD and every screened dataset lack the data. Table 12.1 is the limitation log; Table A.41 (Appendix XV) adds the effect of each
limit. The results describe UK distance-learning modules of 2013–2014, and instructional conditions change what
predicts success [15], so the university's courses need their own recount; confounding makes the effect of contact
unknowable from these data [14], and a flag must not become a label, because predictive analysis carries a potential
for bias and stereotyping [16].

**Table 12.1. Limitation log**

| Limitation | Kind | Handling |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Year difference over all enrolments in shared modules; small-module click signs (intervals include 0) | No evidence found | No claim; reported as uncertain |
| Contact and its outcome; mode, year of study, attendance; capacity | No data to test | Randomised pilot (C04, C05, C11) |
| Several-modules sign before 2014; leave and transfer | No data to test | Described on 2014 (C12); university recount |
| AQ3 probabilities (calibration fails in 8 of 13 runs); active non-completers rarely listed at week 4 | Measurement | Ranks and reasons only; a later review point to test in the pilot |
| 2014 runs described for AQ1 and AQ2 before the AQ3 test | Scope | Design fixed on 2013 (C15); tuning criterion recorded (C18) |
| Binary click signs; below-median sign weak in 2014 once adjusted; KPI proxy and denominator | Measurement | Continuous measures in AQ3; one add-drop rule |
| UK distance learning of 2013–2014; observational design | Scope and causal | Re-check on the university's records; options as hypotheses |
