# 2. Business Understanding and Success Criteria

Our Business Goal is to **improve successful course completion in the program's online and blended courses by
identifying students who stop taking part early enough to support them within the semester**: an outcome for the
organisation, more students passing their courses, rather than a model or a dashboard. The
SMART Goal (specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and time-bound) makes it measurable; the scenario gives no real completion rate, so its
values are assumptions replaced by the real rate before any target is committed:

> **SMART Goal.** Raise the course completion rate of students enrolled after the add-drop period in the pilot
> courses from their recounted baseline (assumed 70%) by five percentage points (assumed target 75%) by the end of
> the 2027–2028 academic year.

The pilot courses, about four courses with about 600 enrolments (both assumptions), are fixed in advance and compared
with similar courses outside the pilot, and the baseline is recounted under Circular 83/2026. The goal meets the five
SMART criteria (Table A.2, Appendix II), but it measures progress, not the effect of contact, which only the randomised
pilot of Section 12.1 can test.
