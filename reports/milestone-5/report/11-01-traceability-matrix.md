## 11.1. Final Project Traceability Matrix

Tables 11.1 and 11.2 form the Final Project Traceability Matrix: they follow each final question in one row from the
goal to the data and on from the preparation to the decision, with GAP or N.A. where a link has no evidence. All rows
share the Business Goal and the SMART Goal of Section 2; Table A.27 (Appendix XI) writes out each cell and Table A.28
justifies each status. For AQ1 and AQ2 every link holds evidence, GAP marking only variables OULAD lacks, and both meet their criteria; for AQ3 and AQ4 the chain
ends at the evaluation design, because the prototype has not been run (Section 10).
