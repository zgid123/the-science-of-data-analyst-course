## 5.1. Requirement–Data Fit and Question Status

In Milestone 3 we rated each question against its data: GREEN when the data are enough, YELLOW when the question must be
narrowed or its claim changed, and RED when a key concept, variable or intervention is missing. Table 5.2 is the
AQ–Data Fit Matrix with the action and the final status (Table A.13, Appendix V, writes out each cell). Missing values
keep a category or an indicator and are never imputed, and missing variables are never simulated (Table A.14). Three
parts of the proposal need the university's own records: the contact part of AQ4, comparisons by year of study,
delivery mode or attendance, and the contact parts of the secondary KPIs; no question is dropped.

**Table 5.2. AQ–Data Fit Matrix**

| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| AQ1 | YELLOW | Year of study, delivery mode; exam results in 2 of 7 modules | TMA and CMA only; day-14 rule; both years (C15) | Modify | Answered |
| AQ2 | YELLOW | Attendance; several-modules sign in 2013 | Signs up to day 27; several-modules sign on 2014 only (C12, C15) | Modify | Answered |
| AQ3 | YELLOW | A task-only rule fires in 1 of 22 runs at day 13 | Rule with inactivity (C10); test on 2014 | Modify | Deferred |
