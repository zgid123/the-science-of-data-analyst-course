## 11.4. AQ3 and AQ4: Expected Outputs

AQ3 and AQ4 depend on a model that has not been trained (Section 10), so their answers are expected outputs, with no
model metric. AQ3 would give a risk rank per enrolment at days 13 and 27, a probability only if calibration holds, and
would be answered by gate S1 on the 13 runs of 2014; if the model does not beat the rule, the rule's order stays, with
its list performance measured as baseline B2. AQ4 would give a list of 3 or 5 per 20 students per run with the signs
behind each name, answered when it meets S1, rule FR1 passes or its flags are reviewed, and the risk order is stated as
untested for need (Table A.37, Appendix XIV). Neither says who should make contact or whether contact helps.
