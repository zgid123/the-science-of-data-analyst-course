All four answers rest on observational data, and OULAD records no contact, so every prescriptive statement is a
decision option with a hypothesis for the pilot (Table A.40). For D1, the AQ3 model ranks the list and the early-sign rule (C10) is its fallback: the rule
is interpretable, computable from records the university already holds and holds 97% of the model's hits, while the
list length, the reasons shown and the pilot's choice of person and offer are set out in Appendix XIV; for D2, early support time goes first where non-completion and early signs
are highest, as a hypothesis for the university's records; and for D3, the project offers a measurement method, a gate
and a fairness audit, while extension waits for a pilot result.
