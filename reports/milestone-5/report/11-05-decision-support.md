All four answers rest on observational data, and OULAD records no contact, so every prescriptive statement is a
decision option with a hypothesis for the pilot (Table A.40). For D1, the early-sign rule (C10) is the candidate list: it is interpretable,
computable from records the university already holds, and each of its parts goes with later non-completion within the
runs of 2013. How well its list of 3 or 5 per 20 reaches later non-completers is measured as baseline B2 of the AQ3
test before D1 relies on it, the AQ3 ranking replaces its order only if gate S1 is met, and the choice of person and
offer stays open for the pilot to randomise; for D2, early support time goes first where non-completion and early signs
are highest, as a hypothesis for the university's records; and for D3, the project offers a measurement method, a gate
and a fairness audit, while extension waits for a pilot result.
