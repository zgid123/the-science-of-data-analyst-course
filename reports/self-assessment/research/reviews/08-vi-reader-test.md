# Reader re-test after the fixes

Overall judgement. Read as a lecturer who grades the course and has only the Vietnamese text, the document does its job: the opening paragraph of Section 1 (01-00:3) says what the document is (a preliminary self-assessment of five milestones plus the group's comments), gives the overall result (45 criteria; 1 not applicable; 35 đạt, 9 đạt phần lớn, 0 đạt một phần) and points to Appendix I and II for detail. The counts agree across Section 1, Table A.2 and the five tables of Appendix II (8+9+10+7+11 = 45; 2+2+1+1+3 = 9). Appendix II reads on its own because its opening paragraph says the section and table numbers point into each milestone's report. Most of the prose reads naturally. The real problems are (a) Table 1.1 is very dense and leans on terms the main text never introduces: the four analytical questions (mô tả / chẩn đoán / dự báo / chỉ định) and the date the contact list is drawn up; (b) three "đạt phần lớn" verdicts give page length as the limitation of a GenAI criterion, and Milestone 4 puts optional Level 2 under "Presentation & consistency", so the limitation does not match the criterion; (c) a few factual tensions a grader would notice: day 13 vs day 14 for the list, "results reported separately" for a predictive question that has not been evaluated yet, and a future-tense "sẽ được hoàn thiện" in a document submitted on 14/11. No passage was completely impenetrable (no clarity 1), but several needed a second reading.
