# Reader test of the restructured document

Reader: Vietnamese-native lecturer of a data-analytics course, first reading of the five `.vi.md` files in file-name order, no access to the English version.

## Overall judgement

Yes, a reader of the field can explain this document afterwards: it is the group's self-assessment of the five milestone reports against each brief's grading criteria, with four levels instead of points, the result is 35 "đạt" and 9 "đạt phần lớn", the recurring weakness is report length, and the detail sits in Appendix I (scale and summary) and Appendix II (criterion by criterion). The opening paragraph of Section 1 says all of this, and Appendix I repeats it, so each appendix also reads on its own. It reads as one whole, but four things break the flow. (1) The count "35 trong 44 tiêu chí" is given twice without saying that one more criterion (Milestone 5) is "không áp dụng", so the total of 45 criteria in Appendix II never matches the headline; I had to add Table A.2 by hand to understand where 44 comes from. (2) The page counts mix languages: Table A.2 gives the English length for Milestones 1–4 but the Vietnamese length for Milestone 5, without saying which, and the sentence after it ("chỉ bản tiếng Anh của báo cáo Milestone 5 nằm trong mức khuyến nghị") depends on the English Milestone 5 length (24 pages), which appears only in Appendix II. (3) Section 1 never names the dataset (OULAD appears only in Appendix II, Table A.4) or what "lượt nhấp", "module" and "kỳ mở" refer to, so Table 1.1 assumes the reader already knows the project; a grader of this course does, but the table does not stand alone. (4) "module" and "học phần" alternate for the same thing, and "nhóm" means both the team and a group of students (Bảng 1.1, line 20), which made me stop. Table 1.1 is dense: most cells pack three or four facts, and the column "Điểm khác với kế hoạch" mostly describes the problem rather than a difference from a plan. Appendix II cells are noun lists without verbs, which is acceptable for evidence, but several cells (Table A.4 row 5, Table A.6 row 4, Table A.7 row 7) carry too many ideas for one reading.
