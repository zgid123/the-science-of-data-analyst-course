# Reader test of the per-milestone restructure

## Reader test (stage 5): Vietnamese lecturer, first reading of the six `.vi.md` files

**Opening judgement.** The document does its job: Section 1 says in its first paragraph what the document is (the group's preliminary self-assessment of five milestones), what OULAD is and what the project aims at, and the closing paragraph of Section 1 gives the overall result (38 of 45 criteria "đạt", 7 "đạt phần lớn"). Table 2.1 is clear, row by row, about what went wrong, how it was handled and where it stands; Appendix I and Appendix II each open with a paragraph that lets them be read alone. The criterion counts agree everywhere (Table 1.1, Appendix I and Tables A.2–A.6 all give 6+2, 8+1, 10, 6+1, 8+3 = 38+7 = 45), and the page counts, AQ1 (45,3%), S1 (41,5% vs 40,3%), 19 issues, 22 checks and 88 variables agree too. What slows a first reading is concentrated in Table 1.1 row 5 and row 4 (dense result cells with model-evaluation shorthand that the reader only decodes later in Table A.6), and in Table 2.1 row "Phép kiểm tra Level 2", whose "điều đã xảy ra" lists three facts without saying why each is a problem. Two substantive mismatches: (a) Table 1.1 row 5 lists a calibration limitation (predicted risk matches the observed rate in only 5 of 13 test presentations) that appears nowhere in Table A.6, so the reader cannot tie it to a criterion; (b) Table 1.1 row 4 says only modelled questions have a baseline, Table A.5 says every question has one. A smaller logic tension: the stakeholder criterion is lowered to "đạt phần lớn" for simulated interviews while the same cell says the brief allows them. In total 26 passages flagged: 0 at clarity 1, 15 at clarity 2, 11 at clarity 3.
