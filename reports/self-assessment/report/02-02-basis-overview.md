## Appendix II – Basis of the Self-Assessment

This appendix gives the basis of our preliminary assessment of the results we delivered on the five milestones of the final project. Each milestone is assessed against the grading criteria that its brief lists, in the same order and wording, so that each row of our assessment answers one criterion the lecturers use to mark the work. The briefs give a total of 100 points per milestone but no points per criterion, so we rate each criterion on the scale in Table A.4 rather than award points.

**Table A.4. Rating scale used in this assessment**

| Rating | Meaning |
| :---- | :---- |
| Met | Every element the criterion names is present in the submitted report, with evidence. |
| Mostly met | The criterion is met, with one limitation that we state in the same row. |
| Partly met | An element the criterion names is missing or rests on evidence weaker than the brief asks for. |
| Not applicable | The brief makes the criterion optional, and we did not take up the option. |

For every criterion, the evidence column of the tables in Appendix III names the section, table or appendix of that milestone's submitted report where a reader finds the work. Page lengths are those of the main text without appendices, counted page by page on the submitted Word documents, in English and in Vietnamese. Table A.1 (Appendix I) summarises the five milestones: the task each one assigned, what we delivered and the number of criteria at each rating.

Of the 45 criteria across the five milestones, 37 are met and 8 are mostly met; none is rated partly met, and none is not applicable, because Milestone 5 ran the optional Level 2 prototype. Length lowers a rating only where a criterion grades presentation or report quality, in Milestones 1, 4 and 5; the Milestone 2 and 3 rubrics have no criterion on presentation, so their length is recorded in Table A.1 but lowers no rating. For Milestone 4, whose brief sets no page range, the length is measured against the 6–10 or 8–12 pages recommended by the briefs of Milestones 1–3. Of the four briefs that recommend a length, only the English report of Milestone 5, at about 25 pages, stays within it, because we wrote each report in full and moved only detail beyond what the brief requires into the appendices. The criteria of each milestone are assessed in Appendix III, and the problems we met along the way, with how we solved them, are in Section 1.
