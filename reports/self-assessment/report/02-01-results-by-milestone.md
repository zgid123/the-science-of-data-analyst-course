## Appendix I – Results by Milestone

This appendix supports Section 1. Table A.1 follows the project milestone by milestone: the task each brief assigned, what we delivered, what the milestone added to the project story and how far it met its brief. We rated each grading criterion that the briefs list, in their own order and wording, as met, mostly met, partly met or not applicable, because the briefs give 100 points per milestone but no points per criterion (Appendix II). The last column gives the number of criteria at each rating and the limitations behind them; Appendix III rates every criterion with its evidence in the submitted report. The briefs also ask that the five reports form one project rather than five separate pieces of work. Table A.2 gives the answer that Milestone 5 reached for each analytical question, and Table A.3 then assesses that coherence on four aspects, from the goal that runs through the milestones to the changes made against the plan.

**Table A.2. Answers to the four analytical questions in the Milestone 5 report**

| Question | Answer |
| :---- | :---- |
| AQ1 (descriptive) | Of the 28,061 enrolments still registered on day 14, the reference day of the completion indicator (the contact lists themselves are drawn up on days 13 and 27), 45.3% did not complete. |
| AQ2 (diagnostic) | Five early signs hold in both years: a missed task, a late tutor-marked assignment, no click in the last seven days, activity below the median of the run and no click before the course start. Students who missed a task show a non-completion rate about 40 percentage points higher than students who missed none. |
| AQ3 (predictive) | A model built on the 2013 runs and tested once on the 13 runs of 2014 ranks students better than the early-sign rule. Lists of 5 students in every 20 hold 41.5% of later non-completers, against 40.3% for the rule; the gain is small but beyond chance in a paired test, so gate S1 is met. The predicted risks match the observed rates in only 5 of the 13 test runs, so staff see ranks rather than probabilities. |
| AQ4 (prescriptive) | Each list gives a reason for every name; in each tutor group of 20 students (the students one tutor supports), the first student contacted turns out to be a later non-completer 92.4% of the time; and the fairness rule finds no group of students at a disadvantage. |

Across the five milestones, 37 of the 45 criteria are met and 8 are mostly met; none is rated partly met. The limitation that recurs is length: only the English report of Milestone 5 stays within its brief's recommended length, because we wrote each report in full and moved only detail beyond what the brief requires into the appendices. Length lowers a rating only where a criterion grades presentation or report quality, in Milestones 1, 4 and 5 (Appendix II).
