## 1.2. Scope of Milestone 2 and Change Log Summary

**What this milestone does.** Building on the frozen baseline, this milestone answers the two questions that the brief
sets: which data the four questions need, and whether the open dataset that we collect actually provides them [1]. Its
work follows the brief's steps from the data requirements (Section 2) to the coverage of each question by the real
files and variables (Section 7), and covers the risks and the use of generative artificial intelligence (AI) (Sections
8 and 9). Cleaning the data, filling in missing values, building features and training models are left to later
milestones, as the brief requires [1].

**What the data changed.** The selected dataset, the Open University Learning Analytics Dataset (OULAD), describes
distance-learning modules of a university in the United Kingdom (UK), not the courses of a Vietnamese program, so the
questions cannot be answered on it exactly as the proposal framed them. We recorded eight controlled revisions,
C01 to C08, each with its data evidence. OULAD stands in for the university's courses in the analysis (C01); a semester
becomes a module run, one offering of a module lasting eight to nine months, with time counted in days from the run's
start (C02); and the action part of AQ4 stays a requirement for the pilot (C08). Section 7.2 explains every revision,
Appendix V (Table A.9) gives the full Change Log, and Appendix VII (item 1) describes all eight.

None of the revisions alters the Business Goal, the SMART Goal or the decisions. They narrow how far each question can
be answered on open data, and they keep the full questions as the target for the university's own records.
