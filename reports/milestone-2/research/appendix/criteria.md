Every candidate was scored on the ten criteria of the brief [1]. They ask whether the
dataset can answer the questions (AQ coverage), whether its population resembles the project's (population fit),
whether its unit and time grain match (unit and granularity), whether it holds the outcome (outcome availability),
whether its predictors exist before the prediction point (feature availability and timing), whether it covers enough
time (temporal coverage), whether it is documented and traceable (documentation and provenance), whether it may be
used (license and access), whether it can be handled (quality feasibility) and whether it is anonymised and
representative (ethics and representativeness). Each criterion takes 0 when it is not met or not stated, 1 when it is
partly met or uncertain, and 2 when it is well met.
