# S307 – HarvardX Person-Course Academic Year 2013 De-Identified dataset, version 3.0 (Harvard Dataverse)

## Summary in my own words

The Harvard Dataverse record (doi:10.7910/DVN/26147, first published 27 May 2014, dataset version V11 released 18 December 2019, data release "version 3.0" created 12 November 2019) holds de-identified person-course records from the first year of HarvardX courses on edX (Fall 2012, Spring 2013, Summer 2013). Each record aggregates one individual's activity in one course: flags for registered, viewed, explored and certified, a grade, first and last event dates (start_time_DI, last_event_DI), counts of events, active days, video plays, chapters and forum posts, plus country, education level, year of birth and gender. The DDI metadata show 338,223 non-missing values for several variables, i.e. the record count of the release. There are no dated assessments, no daily activity and no withdrawal outcome; certification is the only completion measure. No open licence is named; use is governed by the Dataverse Community Norms, which forbid re-identification. The landing page is JavaScript-only; the DDI metadata export was read instead.

## Key facts

- Period and platform – "This release is comprised of de-identified data from the first year (Academic Year 2013: Fall 2012, Spring 2013, and Summer 2013) of HarvardX courses on the edX platform along with related documentation."
- Unit – "These data are aggregate records, and each record represents one individual's activity in one edX course."
- Release date – "The first release of this dataset is the HarvardX Person-Course Academic Year 2013 De-Identified dataset, version 3.0, created on November 12, 2019."
- Checksum – "The md5sum for this release (HXPC13_DI_v3_11-13-2019.csv) is: 53419b486c3b19c14d2f06612980f630"
- Terms – "Users of the dataset agree to abide by the Dataverse Community Norms, specifically:"
- No re-identification – "produce and/or publish connections among datasets that could identify individuals or organizations; or"
- Variables include certified, grade, start_time_DI, last_event_DI, ndays_act (variable names in the DDI export: "start_time_DI" and "last_event_DI")
- Documentation files – "Person Course Deidentification.pdf"
- Works cited: Ho A. et al. (HarvardX and MITx Working Paper 2014) – "HarvardX and MITx: The first year of open online courses"
