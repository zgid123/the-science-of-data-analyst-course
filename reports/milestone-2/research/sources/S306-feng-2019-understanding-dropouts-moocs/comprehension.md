# S306 – Understanding Dropouts in MOOCs (Feng, Tang & Liu, AAAI 2019)

## Summary in my own words

The AAAI 2019 paper by Tsinghua University authors studies dropout on XuetangX, one of the largest Chinese MOOC platforms, and proposes the CFIN model to predict it. Only the abstract and the bibliographic record were readable (the full PDF sits behind a captcha; the crawl is a Wayback copy of the landing page). The abstract confirms that the data come from XuetangX, that dropouts correlate across courses and between friends, and that experiments ran on two large datasets. The paper is the citation the MOOCData page (S305) requests.
