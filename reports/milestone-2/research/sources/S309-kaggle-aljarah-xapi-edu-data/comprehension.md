# S309 – Students' Academic Performance Dataset (xAPI-Edu-Data), Kaggle listing by I. Aljarah

## Summary in my own words

The dataset is listed on Kaggle by Ibrahim Aljarah, one of its three authors at the University of Jordan; the group's own resources page (S308) refused connections, so the Kaggle record (read through Kaggle's public metadata API with WebFetch, quotes checked against the raw JSON) is the closest provider-controlled source. It holds 480 student records with 16 attributes collected from the Kalboard 360 learning management system through the xAPI tracker, 245 from a first semester and 235 from a second, with students from several countries (179 from Kuwait, 172 from Jordan). The target groups students by total grade into Low, Middle and High levels; there is no withdrawal outcome and no dated record. The licence on Kaggle is CC BY-SA 4.0 and users are asked to cite two papers by Amrieh, Hamtini and Aljarah (2015, 2016).
