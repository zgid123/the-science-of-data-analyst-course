# S304 – Higher Education Students Performance Evaluation [Dataset], UCI Machine Learning Repository (Yilmaz & Şekeroğlu)

## Summary in my own words

The UCI record (DOI 10.24432/C51G82, donated 14 August 2023) holds a questionnaire of 145 students of the Faculty of Engineering and the Faculty of Educational Sciences collected in 2019, with the stated aim of predicting end-of-term performance. Thirty answers cover personal, family and study-habit questions (including salary, partner and parental status) plus a course ID, and the output is a letter grade from Fail to AA. There is no withdrawal category, no date or activity log and a single collection year. The creators' e-mail domain (neu.edu.tr) and the question on accommodation in Cyprus place it at a university in Cyprus; the page does not name the institution. The licence is CC BY 4.0; the file is one 10.8 KB CSV.
