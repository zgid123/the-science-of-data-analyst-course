# S303 – Student Performance [Dataset], UCI Machine Learning Repository (Cortez, 2008)

## Summary in my own words

The UCI record (DOI 10.24432/C5TG7T, donated 26 November 2014) holds two tables of secondary-school students from two Portuguese schools, one for Mathematics and one for Portuguese language, collected from school reports and questionnaires. Each row is a student in one subject with 30 demographic, social and school attributes and three period grades (G1, G2, G3 on a 0–20 scale), G3 being the final-year target. There is no withdrawal outcome, no dated task or activity, and only one school year is implied. Several attributes are sensitive (alcohol consumption, health, romantic relationship, family status). The licence is CC BY 4.0 and the files are a 20 KB zip.
