# 9. Generative AI Usage and Reflection

Generative AI supported this milestone in four of the roles the brief allows, and no fact about a dataset was accepted
from it without checking the official source [1]. Table 9.1 lists each use with its control; Appendix VI holds the
prompts, the critiques and the team's decisions.

**Table 9.1. Roles of generative AI and their controls**

| Role (brief) | How AI was used | Verification |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Search strategist | An AI agent working for the team turned the requirement keywords into queries and opened repositories and provider pages, using the prompt in Appendix VI | Every query and hit logged (Appendix I); every fact saved with a quoted passage from the official page and checked by team members against that page |
| Metadata interpreter | The same agent summarised units of analysis, outcomes and licenses from data descriptors and repository pages | Facts kept only with a verbatim quotation; facts the pages left open checked in the files (Section 6) |
| Requirement critic | An isolated AI critic reviewed the data requirements before any dataset was scored | 12 critiques: 10 accepted, 2 in part (Appendix VI) |
| Selection reviewer | An isolated AI reviewer challenged the scoring and the decision | 10 critiques, all accepted (Appendix VI) |

The two critics changed the work in ways the team could check. The requirement critic showed that the gates must require a
dated withdrawal and dated tasks, because AQ3 and secondary KPI 1 need both; the gates require them, and they were
settled before any candidate was scored. Its demand that year of study and delivery mode be
must-have was accepted only in part. No open dataset holds them, and a gate that no dataset can pass would leave only
synthetic data, which the brief forbids [1]; the clauses that depend on them were declared Partial instead. The
selection reviewer found that OULAD's first scores rested partly on facts its documentation does not state, such as
its outcome values and the number of runs per module; the scores were lowered to what was recorded, and the choice
was made conditional on the structural check, which confirmed two of the three conditions.

AI was not used to create data, statistics or license information. Every number in Sections 5 to 7 comes from the collection
and verification scripts, which read the files themselves, and every dataset fact in Sections 3 and 4 from a saved
official page.
