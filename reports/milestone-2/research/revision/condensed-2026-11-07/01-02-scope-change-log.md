## 1.2. Scope of Milestone 2 and Change Log Summary

Building on that baseline, this milestone answers two questions: which data the four questions need, and whether the
collected open dataset provides them [1]. Its work covers the data requirements (Section 2), the search for and
scoring of candidate datasets (Sections 3 and 4), the reproducible collection of the selected dataset (Section 5), a
structural check of the files (Section 6), and the coverage of each question by real variables (Section 7). Data
cleaning, imputation, feature construction and modelling are left to later milestones [1].

The selected dataset, the Open University Learning Analytics Dataset (OULAD), changes the proposal in eight places,
each recorded with its evidence in the Controlled Change Log (Appendix V). Four of them matter most. The population
changes, since OULAD describes United Kingdom (UK) distance-learning modules from 2013–2014 rather than a Vietnamese program's courses (C01). The time frame changes,
since each module run lasts about nine months and is counted in days from its start (C02). Comparisons by delivery
mode and year of study are dropped, because the data do not hold them (C04). The action part of AQ4 stays a
requirement for the pilot, because no open dataset records it (C08). None of the changes alters the Business Goal,
the SMART Goal or the decisions; they narrow how far each question can be answered on open data.
