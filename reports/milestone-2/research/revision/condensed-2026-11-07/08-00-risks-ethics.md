# 8. Risks, Ethics and Limitations

The collected data are open and anonymised, but they still describe real students, and the gaps of Section 7 limit
what the later milestones may claim. Their use is bound by privacy and ethics conditions, and the data themselves set
limits on what can be concluded.

OULAD was anonymised before release: identifiers were randomised, dates are days relative to each run, and the data
meet k-anonymity with k = 5 [3]. It still holds sensitive attributes, among them the deprivation band of the
student's area and declared disability [3]. The project uses them only to check that associations and list
accuracy hold across groups, never as reasons to list a student, and it makes no attempt to re-identify anyone. The
data stay on the team's machines under their CC BY 4.0 license [2], with the descriptor cited, and no record is
entered into public artificial intelligence (AI) tools.

Representativeness is the largest limit. The data come from UK distance learners in 2013–2014, while the proposal
concerns a Vietnamese program's online and blended courses; their modules were also chosen partly because many
students failed them [3], so their failure and withdrawal rates are likely to be higher than a typical program's. Any rate,
association or accuracy measured on OULAD therefore describes OULAD and has to be re-checked on the university's own
records before it informs a decision (C01). The module runs last about nine months rather than one semester (C02), and
module runs starting in February and in October differ in structure [3], so findings may not carry over between
them.

Several claims cannot be supported by these data. No causal statement about engagement and completion follows from
observational records, and nothing about the effect of contacting students can be learned, because no contact or
intervention is recorded (C08). Comparisons by delivery mode and year of study are impossible (C04). Three data issues
also need decisions in the next milestone rather than silent fixes: 787,170 identical click rows, 93 withdrawals
without a date and, as the descriptor states, the exam results missing for most modules [3]. Each will be handled in the open, with its effect on
counts reported.
