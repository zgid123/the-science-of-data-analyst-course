## 1.1. Frozen Baseline

Before any dataset was examined, the Milestone 1 proposal was frozen: its goal, measures, questions, data needs and
decisions were copied verbatim and the source files' checksums recorded, so that every later change is visible
(Appendix V). The brief requires this step because the questions, not the data, decide what is collected [1].

The Business Goal is to improve successful course completion in a university program's online and blended courses by
identifying students who stop taking part early enough to support them within the semester. The SMART (specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and time-bound) Goal
sets the measure. It raises the completion rate of students enrolled after the add-drop period in the pilot courses
from 70% to 75% by the end of the 2027–2028 academic year; both figures are assumptions of the scenario. Approved leave
and transfers are left out of the count, as Vietnam's Circular 83/2026 does when it counts students who leave a program. The primary key performance indicator (KPI) is
the course completion rate. Secondary KPI 1 is the early-sign contact rate: the share of students
showing early signs by week 4 who are contacted by the end of week 5. Secondary KPI 2 is the follow-up rate: the share
of listed students contacted within seven days of the list.
Table 1.1 lists the four analytical questions (AQ) with their analytics type and the decision each supports.

**Table 1.1. Frozen AQ1–AQ4 with analytics types and decisions**

| AQ | Analytics type | Question in short | Decision supported |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| AQ1 | Descriptive | Shares of students who pass, fail or withdraw, by course, delivery mode and year of study over four semesters, and when the first assessed task is missed | D2: where the program places advisors' time; D3: whether to extend after the pilot |
| AQ2 | Diagnostic | Which signs in weeks 1–4 (missed or late tasks, learning-platform activity, tasks missed in several courses, attendance) go with later non-completion | D2; the reasons shown in D1 |
| AQ3 | Predictive | How well non-completion can be estimated from data recorded by the end of week 2 and week 4, against a simple rule based on missed assessed tasks | D1: who is listed in weeks 3 and 5 |
| AQ4 | Prescriptive | Which students to list, in what order and with which contact, within weekly staff capacity and fairness limits | D1: whom to contact, by whom, with which offer |
