## 3.2. Missingness and Validity

Missing values are few, and their pattern shows where they come from. The deprivation band is blank for 1,111
enrolments (3.41%), concentrated in North Region and Ireland, and across all 32,593 enrolments these students pass,
with or without distinction, in 65.7% of cases against 46.6% of the others, so the blanks relate to the outcome and
are kept as their own group rather than filled (DQ12); 45 registration days and 173 TMA scores are also missing (DQ13,
DQ11), and three gaps are structural rather than lost values: an empty unregistration day, a missing submission row
and the exam results absent by design for 18 of the 24 exams (DQ08) [2]. The validity checks found scores within 0 to
100 and no submission before registration, but six inconsistencies: 93 Withdrawn enrolments without an unregistration
day (DQ04); 58 registrations after the two-week sign-up window (DQ05); 1,909 banked results dated −1 (DQ07); 98,745
clicks and 588 submissions dated after the student unregistered (DQ03); 32,991 of the 69,956 CMA submissions after
their due day, although the OU's policy of 2025 accepts no late CMA [3] (DQ10); and one band label without its percent
sign (DQ12). None is corrected by changing a recorded value; each receives a flag or a rule in Section 5. Appendix I
gives the full evidence of this section.
