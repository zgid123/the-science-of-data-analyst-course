## 7.3. Residual Risks

Five issues remain in the prepared data on purpose, because resolving them needs information that OULAD does not
hold; the first three are flagged with their size measured, so that Milestone 4 can report results with and without
them, and the last two are limits of the KPI and of the capacity scenarios. They are the exact duplicate click rows,
which would remove 3.19% of all clicks; the late CMAs of undocumented meaning, kept out of the early-sign rule, and
the CMA marks known by the cut-off, which cover only 1.4% of training rows at day 13 and 5.2% at day 27, below the
10% coverage we require of a predictor, so the CMA score is prepared but not used; the 93 Withdrawn enrolments
without a withdrawal day and the 1,962 test enrolments of students seen in 2013; the KPI as a proxy, with completion
at day 14 of 54.9% instead of 54.7% without the 138 flagged enrolments; and the assumed caseloads behind the list
sizes, which are scenarios rather than plans. Appendix VI gives each risk with its evidence and handling.
