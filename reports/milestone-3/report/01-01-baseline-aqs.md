## 1.1. Baseline and Current Analytical Questions

**Baseline.** The baseline is the Milestone 1 proposal as revised in Milestone 2. Its Business Goal is to improve
successful course completion in a university program's online and blended courses by identifying students who stop
taking part early enough to support them within the semester. The
specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and time-bound (SMART) Goal raises the completion rate of students enrolled after the add-drop period in the pilot courses
from an assumed 70% to 75% by the end of the 2027–2028 academic year; completion means passing the course, and a fail
or a withdrawal counts as non-completion. Three key performance indicators (KPI) track the goal: the completion rate
itself (primary KPI); the share of students showing early signs of disengagement who are contacted by the end of week
5 (secondary KPI 1), the signs being set by the early-sign rule of missed or late assessed tasks by the end of week 4;
and the share of listed students contacted within seven days (secondary KPI 2). The proposal supports three
decisions: whom the lecturer-advisors and the support office contact at the review points of weeks 3 and 5, each list
using the data up to its data cut-off at the end of week 2 or week 4 (D1); where the program manager places the time of
these staff (D2); and whether to extend the approach to all courses after the pilot (D3). Milestone 2 selected the
Open University Learning Analytics Dataset (OULAD) as an open stand-in for the university's own records, which cannot be used yet, and
recorded eight changes, C01 to C08, in the Controlled Change Log: they set the population to the OULAD module runs of
2013–2014, the cut-offs to day 13 and day 27, the KPI denominator to the enrolments still registered on day 14 and the
test to the 2014 runs of models built on 2013, and on OULAD the early-sign rule counts one missed or late task (C06).
Appendix IV gives the goal, the KPIs, the decisions and each change in full.

**The questions as they stand.** Table 1.1 gives the four analytical questions (AQ) after the changes of Milestone 2
and the data gap decisions of this milestone, with the type of analytics each belongs to and the decision each
supports.

**Table 1.1. AQ1–AQ4 as they stand**

| AQ | Analytics type | Current question | Decision supported |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| AQ1 | Descriptive | What share of enrolments end in pass, fail or withdrawal, by module, run, start month and year, and in which week non-completers first miss an assessed task? | D2: where to place support time; D3: whether to extend |
| AQ2 | Diagnostic | Which signs recorded by the end of week 4 (missed and late tasks, inactivity, activity against the run's median) go with later non-completion, compared within each module? | D2, and the reasons shown with each name in D1 |
| AQ3 | Predictive | How well can non-completion be estimated from the data up to the end of week 2 and of week 4, compared with the early-sign rule, when a model built on the 2013 runs is applied to the 2014 runs? | D1: who is listed |
| AQ4 | Prescriptive (ranking) | Which enrolments should rank first on a list of an assumed capacity, with what gain over a random list and what recall, overall and by deprivation band and disability? | D1: whom to list first; the choice of contact is deferred to the pilot |

The Business Goal, the SMART Goal and the three decisions are the same as in Milestone 1. The data understanding of
this milestone led to five further changes, C09 to C13, which narrow how the questions are measured on OULAD without
changing what they ask; Section 4 explains each of them, and Appendix IV gives the full Change Log.
