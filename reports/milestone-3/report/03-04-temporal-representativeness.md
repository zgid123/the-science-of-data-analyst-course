## 3.4. Temporal Coverage and Representativeness

The data cover the February and October runs of 2013 and 2014, with activity from about four weeks before the start
to week 39 (Appendix I, Figure A.2); two days near the end of all six February 2014 runs carry no click at all, a
logging gap treated as missing (DQ15). The cut-offs sit early: a TMA or CMA falls due by day 13 in only 1 of the 22
runs and by day 27 in 16, so a rule that counts missed tasks has nothing to count at the first cut-off in 21 runs
(DQ18). For prediction on a later run, 9 pairs of module and start month ran in both years, and only withdrawals dated
by a cut-off can be known at it [8]. Representativeness limits every claim: OULAD is a subset of OU students of
2013–2014, reduced by anonymisation, with modules chosen partly because many students failed them [2], and completion
in the KPI population ranges from 45.7% in CCC to 73.8% in AAA. Results therefore describe OULAD runs, groups are
compared within module, and every result must be checked on the university's own records before it informs decisions
D1 to D3 (DQ19). Appendix I gives the full evidence of this section.
