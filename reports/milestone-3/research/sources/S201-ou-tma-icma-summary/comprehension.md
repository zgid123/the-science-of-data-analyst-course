# S201 – The Open University (2025). TMA and iCMA Policy: Summary

## Summary in my own words

The Open University (OU) summary of its Tutor-Marked Assignment (TMA) and interactive Computer-Marked Assignment (iCMA) policy explains that both are the continuous assessments of a module. Submissions are due at midday UK time on the cut-off date with a grace period to 23:59. TMAs are marked by the student's tutor, who usually aims to return them within ten working days of the cut-off date (three to four weeks for postal submissions), and tutors may collect a TMA before the cut-off date. iCMAs are marked automatically, cannot be submitted after the cut-off date and have no extensions; their score should be visible on the module website, and students contact the Assessment Processing Centre if it is not available within 7 days of the cut-off date. For OULAD this means a TMA score is known to staff only some working days after the assessment's cut-off day, while a CMA score is available at or shortly after the cut-off day.

## Key facts

- Continuous assessment – "most commonly known as Tutor-Marked Assignments (TMAs) and interactive Computer-Marked Assignments (iCMAs)"
- Deadline time – "The TMA and iCMA should be submitted before the 12:00 hrs (midday UK local time) on the submission cut-off date."
- Grace period – "A 12-hour grace period allows submissions until 23:59 on the same day."
- TMA extensions possible – "If unforeseen circumstances prevent you from submitting your TMA on time, you may request an extension."
- No iCMA after cut-off – "iCMAs cannot be submitted after the cut-off date and it is not possible to request an extension for an iCMA."
- Tutor may collect early – "Please note that tutor can pick up your TMA for marking before the cut-off date."
- TMA marking time – "Tutors usually aim to mark and return your TMA within ten working days of the cut-off date."
- Postal TMA – "you should receive the marked TMA back within three to four weeks of the cut-off date."
- iCMA automatic marking – "iCMAs are marked automatically."
- iCMA score availability – "If your iCMA score is not available within 7 days of the cut-off date"
- Policy date – "Short summary for TMA and iCMA Policy effective from July 2025"
