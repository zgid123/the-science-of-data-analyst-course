# S202 – The Open University Help Centre: Get to know the OU

## Summary in my own words

The OU Help Centre page explains study intensity in credits. Part-time study means 60 credits a year (one 60-credit module or two 30-credit modules) and needs about 16–18 study hours a week; full-time study means 120 credits a year and needs about 32–36 hours a week. The OU advises students with a full-time job to take no more than 60 credits a year. For OULAD this gives a reading of `studied_credits`: 60 is a typical part-time load, 120 a full-time load, and values above 120 an unusually heavy load.

## Key facts

- Part-time hours – "If you want to study part-time, you'll need around 16–18 hours study time per week."
- Part-time credits – "Part-time study means completing 60 credits worth of study per year, for example, one 60 credit module, or two 30 credit modules."
- Full-time hours – "Full-time requires around 32–36 hours study time per week."
- Full-time credits – "It means completing 120 credits worth of study per year, for example, two 60 credit modules."
- Workers – "If you have a full-time job, we recommend you study no more than 60 credits per year."
- Not first year – "We recommend that you don't study full-time in your first year."
