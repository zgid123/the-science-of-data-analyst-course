## 4.1. AQ–Data Fit Ratings

Each question is rated against the claim it carries after the Milestone 2 changes. Table 4.1 gives the rating, the
evidence, the step of the decision procedure that settles the gap and the resulting change, whose full entry is in
the Controlled Change Log (Appendix IV).

**Table 4.1. AQ–Data Fit Decision Log**

| AQ | Rating | Supported by real data | Missing or weak | Action (step) | Change |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| AQ1 | YELLOW | Result of every enrolment; due days of 182 tasks | Year of study, mode; exams in 5 modules | Derive first missed task (2); narrow (4) | C09 |
| AQ2 | YELLOW | Daily clicks; tasks due by day 27 in 16 runs; concurrent runs in 2014 | Attendance; year of study, mode | Derive signs (2); narrow (4) | C12 |
| AQ3 | YELLOW | Target for every enrolment; 9 run pairs in both years | Rule fires in 1 run of 22; no mark release day | Restate rule (2); CMA marks only (4) | C10 |
| AQ4 | RED | Ranking from AQ3; enrolled counts | Contacts, capacity, outcomes after contact | No dataset (3); rewrite (4); defer contact (5) | C11, C13 |

Two findings change how the questions are measured. Two or more tasks fall due by day 27 in only 1 of the 22 runs,
so the Milestone 1 early-sign rule of two or more missed or late tasks becomes "a task due by the cut-off not
submitted, or a TMA submitted late, or no platform activity in the seven days before the cut-off" (C10). In addition,
the Milestone 1 limit of 50% precision for the AQ4 list says little when 44–45% of students do not complete, so list
quality is measured as the gain over each run's base rate and as recall against the most a list of that length can
reach (C13).
