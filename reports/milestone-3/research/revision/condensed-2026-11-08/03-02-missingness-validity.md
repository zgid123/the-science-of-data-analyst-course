## 3.2. Missingness and Validity

Missing values are few, and their pattern shows their origin. The deprivation band is blank for 1,111 enrolments
(3.41%), concentrated in North Region and Ireland, and these students succeed in 65.7% of cases against 46.6% of the other students, so the
blanks relate to the outcome (DQ12); 45 registration days, 39 of them of Withdrawn students, and 173 TMA scores are
missing. Three gaps are structural: an empty unregistration day marks a student who did not withdraw [2], a missing
submission row a task not submitted [2], and exam results are usually absent by design [2], so 18 of 24 exams
have no result row (DQ08).

Validity checks found scores within 0 to 100 and no submission before registration, but six inconsistencies: 93
Withdrawn enrolments without an unregistration day, 87 of them in October 2014 runs (DQ04); 58 registrations after the
two-week sign-up window [2] (DQ05); 1,909 banked results dated −1 (DQ07); 98,745 clicks and 588 submissions after
unregistering (DQ03); 32,991 of 69,956 CMA submissions after their due day, although the OU's 2025 policy accepts no
late CMA [3] (DQ10); and one band label without its percent sign.
