# 7. Prepared Data Validation

The brief asks the team to show that the prepared data serve the analysis better while staying correct and traceable
[1]. The validation script runs 22 checks under fifteen identifiers, V01 to V15, against the raw files and the
profile, and all 22 pass (Appendix VI); Sections 7.1 to 7.3 report the comparisons, the documentation and the risks
that remain.
