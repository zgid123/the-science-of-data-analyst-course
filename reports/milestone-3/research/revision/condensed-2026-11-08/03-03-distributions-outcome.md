## 3.3. Distributions, Outliers and Outcome Balance

Activity is strongly skewed: per enrolment the median is 602 clicks, 3,365 enrolments have none, 2,503 lie beyond
the upper interquartile fence, and a single record reaches 6,977 clicks. These are heavy users rather than errors, so
no value is removed or capped (DQ16). Scores lean the other way, with a median of 80, and 45.8% of CMA scores fall on a
multiple of ten against 17.4% of TMA scores, a sign of coarse marking steps; study load reaches 655 credits against a
99th percentile of 240.

The two outcome classes are close in size. Of the 28,061 enrolments registered by day 14 and not unregistered by it,
the KPI population, 54.7% completed; non-completion reaches 45.3% at day 13 and 44.2% at day 27, ranges from 24.9% to
58.3% between runs, and the smaller class holds at least 92 enrolments in every run. Figure 3.1 shows the results of
each run: withdrawal is highest in CCC (43–46%), and GGG has the fewest withdrawals but the most failures.
