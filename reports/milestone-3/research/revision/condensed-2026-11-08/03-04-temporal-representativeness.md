## 3.4. Temporal Coverage and Representativeness

The data cover February and October runs of 2013 and 2014, with activity from three weeks before the start to week 39.
Weekly clicks per enrolled student rise before the start, peak in week 3, just after the first cut-off, and then settle
(Appendix I, Figure A.2); two consecutive days near the end of all six February 2014 runs carry no click at all (DQ15). The cut-offs sit
early: a TMA or CMA falls due by day 13 in 1 of the 22 runs and by day 27 in 16 (DQ18). For prediction on a later run,
9 module and start-month pairs ran in both years, while CCC, February EEE and February GGG ran only in 2014, and only
withdrawals dated by a cut-off can be known at it [8].

Representativeness limits every claim. OULAD is a subset of OU students from 2013–2014, reduced by anonymisation, with
modules chosen partly because many students failed them [2]. The students learn at a distance, 70.4% are under 35
and 63.1% study 60 credits or fewer at once; women form 11.5% of EEE and 88.4% of BBB, and completion ranges from 45.7%
in CCC to 73.8% in AAA. The deprivation band follows each nation's own index [6]. Results therefore describe OULAD
runs and must be checked on the university's records before they inform decisions D1–D3 (DQ19).
