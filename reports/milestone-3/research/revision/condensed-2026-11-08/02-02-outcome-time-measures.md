## 2.2. Outcome, Time and Measurement Semantics

The outcome is final_result, recorded at the end of each run as Pass (12,361), Withdrawn (10,156), Fail (7,052) or
Distinction (3,024); the descriptor calls the withdrawn value "Withdrawal" [2]. It is a real outcome, not a proxy,
observed only at the end of the run, so every feature must be dated before its prediction point. Non-completion, the
target of the questions, is Fail or Withdrawn; completion is Pass or Distinction.

Time is a count of days from day 0, the official start of the run; students may register from a few months before
the start until two weeks after it [2], so negative days are valid. Week n covers days 7(n−1) to 7n−1, which places
the two cut-offs at day 13 (end of week 2) and day 27 (end of week 4). The assessment date is the task's due day,
which the OU calls its cut-off date [2], and all 1,909 banked results, carried over from an earlier run, carry the
placeholder submission day −1 (Appendix I, Table A.2). Scores run from 0 to 100 with 40 as the pass mark [2], but a
TMA is returned by the tutor, usually within ten working days of its due day, and a CMA score is expected within 7
days [3], while OULAD holds no day on which a mark was released. Weights depart from the documented rule [2] in
the three GGG runs, which weight coursework at zero, and the two CCC runs of 2014, which hold two exams; studied
credits count every module taken at the same time [2], 60 a year being part-time and 120 full-time study [4].
