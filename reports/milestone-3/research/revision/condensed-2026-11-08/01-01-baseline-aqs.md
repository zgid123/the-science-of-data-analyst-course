## 1.1. Baseline and Current Analytical Questions

The Business Goal is to improve successful course completion in a university program's online and blended courses
by identifying students who stop taking part early enough to support them within the semester.
The specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and time-bound (SMART) Goal raises the completion rate of students
enrolled after the add-drop period from an assumed 70% to 75% by the end of 2027–2028; that rate is the primary 
key performance indicator (KPI). Secondary KPI 1 is the share of students showing early signs by week 4 who are
contacted by the end of week 5, and secondary KPI 2 the share of listed students contacted within seven days. The
proposal supports three decisions: who is listed for contact (D1), where advisors' time goes (D2) and whether to
extend after the pilot (D3). Milestone 2 selected the Open University Learning Analytics Dataset (OULAD) and recorded
eight changes, C01–C08, in the Controlled Change Log, among them a population of OULAD module runs of 2013–2014 and
time counted in days from the start of each run.

Table 1.1 gives the four analytical questions (AQ) as they stand after those changes and the data gap decisions of
Section 4, with the decision each supports.

**Table 1.1. AQ1–AQ4 as they stand**

| AQ | Analytics type | Current question | Decision supported |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| AQ1 | Descriptive | Pass, fail and withdrawal shares by module, run, start month and year; week of the first missed task | D2, D3 |
| AQ2 | Diagnostic | Signs by the end of week 4 that go with later non-completion, within each module | D2, D1 reasons |
| AQ3 | Predictive | How well non-completion is estimated by the end of weeks 2 and 4, against an early-sign rule, on a later run | D1 |
| AQ4 | Prescriptive (ranking) | Which enrolments rank first under an assumed capacity, with which gain over chance and recall, overall and by deprivation band and disability | D1; contact deferred |

The Business Goal, the SMART Goal and the three decisions are unchanged. Changes C09–C13 narrow how the questions are
measured on OULAD (Section 4; Appendix IV).
