# 4. AQ–Data Fit and Data Gap Decisions

After data understanding, the brief requires every question to be rated again against the real data: GREEN when
variables, population, time window and quality support the claim, YELLOW when support is partial and the question
must be narrowed or completed, and RED when a key concept, target or intervention is missing [1]. Data gaps are
handled in five steps, in order: (1) check the documentation, (2) derive the variable from real fields, (3) add a
compatible open dataset, (4) narrow or rewrite the question, and (5) only then defer or drop it; synthetic data never fills the gap [1].
Section 4.1 rates the four questions, and Section 4.2 records the gap decisions.
