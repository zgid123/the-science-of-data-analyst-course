## 2.3. Categorical Codes and Sensitive Attributes

The categorical fields use codes that the descriptor explains, and none holds an "unknown" value; the only gap is a
blank deprivation band, and one band label, "10-20", lacks its percent sign. The band, imd_band, is the 
Index of Multiple Deprivation (IMD) band of the student's home area [2]: the English index ranks 32,844 small areas into ten
deciles and describes the area rather than the person [5], and Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland use their own,
not directly comparable indices [6]. Six attributes describe protected or vulnerable groups, namely gender, age
band, declared disability, deprivation band, region and highest education; the smallest groups are those aged 55 or
over (216 enrolments) and postgraduates (313), and 18 age groups and 29 education groups within single runs hold fewer
than 20 students (Appendix I, Table A.3). All six stay in every prepared view for auditing (Section 9).
