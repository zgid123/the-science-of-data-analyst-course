## 1.2. Dataset, Version and Entry Gate

The brief admits a team to Milestone 3 only with a minimum Data Collection Package: the Milestone 1 baseline, an
updated traceability matrix, immutable raw files, a data dictionary, collection evidence and a Controlled Change Log
[1]. All six exist (Appendix I, Table A.1). The raw data are the seven comma-separated values (CSV) tables of OULAD,
figshare version 1 with digital object identifier (DOI) 10.6084/m9.figshare.5081998.v1, and each matches the
Secure Hash Algorithm 256 (SHA-256) checksum pinned in Milestone 2; the eight files of the Milestone 1 proposal that
the baseline was copied from are also unchanged. In the Prepared Data Package the raw tables are hard links to those files, set read-only and checked
again at every run, so they cannot change unnoticed. The dataset holds 22 module runs of seven Open University (OU)
modules from 2013 and 2014 with 32,593 student records [2], which are enrolments of 28,785 distinct students
(Section 2.1). It supports AQ1 to AQ3 in part and the ranking part of AQ4, rather than only a small part of the
proposal, so the entry gate of the brief holds and preparation is justified [1]; the contact part of AQ4 is
deferred (Section 4.1).
