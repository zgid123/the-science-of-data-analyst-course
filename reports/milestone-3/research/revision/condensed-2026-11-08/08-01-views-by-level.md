## 8.1. Prepared Views by Analytics Level

Each analytics level has its own prepared view, and each adds to the one before it (Appendix III, Table A.11). The
descriptive views, enrolment, assessment_due and student_week, give rates on the right denominator without double
counting. The diagnostic view, features_d27, adds the signs at day 27 for comparison within module. The predictive
views add a second cut-off at day 13, the time split and model-ready matrices fitted on training runs, with at least 92
enrolments in the smaller class of every run. The prescriptive table, capacity_by_run, supplies only list sizes under
assumed capacities, because the data hold no action, cost or outcome after contact, and the brief rules out raising a
predictive result to a prescriptive one by advice [1].
