# 3. Data Profiling and Quality

The profile covers the ten aspects the brief lists, from shape and schema to representativeness, each with evidence
from the data [1]. It ran on all 10.9 million rows of the seven raw tables, and thirteen of its figures agree with
an independent recount of the same files by separate code (Appendix I, Table A.5). Section 3.5 gathers the findings of
Sections 3.1 to 3.4 into the Data Quality Issue Log.
