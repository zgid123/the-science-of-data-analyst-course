## 7.3. Residual Risks

Five issues remain in the prepared data on purpose, because resolving them needs information outside OULAD; each is
flagged and measured. (1) Exact duplicate click rows may be copies or repeated visits; the de-duplicated twins bound
their effect at 3.19% of all clicks. (2) The meaning of a late CMA in 2013–2014 is not documented, and the OU marking
policy on record dates from 2025 [3]; the rule therefore uses TMA lateness, while CMA marks known by the cut-off cover
1.4% and 5.2% of training rows, below the 10% coverage a feature needs to enter the Milestone 4 predictor set. (3) 93
Withdrawn enrolments have no withdrawal day and 1,962 test enrolments belong to students seen in 2013; both are
flagged for separate reporting. (4) The KPI is a proxy, since module-run completion stands for course completion and
leave or transfer cannot be told from withdrawal; without the 138 enrolments flagged for an unknown withdrawal (93) or
registration day (45), completion at day 14 is 54.9% instead of 54.7%. (5) The capacity scenarios assume a tutor group
of about 20 students per advisor caseload, a figure from the data descriptor.
